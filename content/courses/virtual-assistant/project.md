## The situation

Fatuma owns a small events business in Nakuru (tents, chairs and sound). She is always busy and has asked a new virtual assistant to do a **paid trial** before hiring. She sends you the made-up information below. Your job is to organise it, show your work and report back like a professional.

> **Note:** All names, numbers and emails here are made up. Do **not** use real phone numbers, email addresses or ID numbers in your work. If you share a Google file, set it to "anyone with the link can view" and check it opens in a private window.

```text
Client: Fatuma's Events, Nakuru
Task notes from Fatuma (Monday):
- Wedding for Achieng and Brian, Saturday 6 December, Naivasha. Need 200 chairs, 20 tables, 1 PA system.
- Graduation party, Sunday 14 December, Nakuru. 80 chairs, 1 tent.
- Church fundraiser, Friday 12 December. Quote requested, no details yet.
Stock: chairs 400 (KES 20 per day each), tables 40 (KES 100 per day each), tent 5 (KES 3,000 per day each).
Please: (1) organise the bookings, (2) work out the hire cost for the two confirmed events, (3) book a call with a supplier in London at 3 pm his time next Tuesday, (4) make a simple Instagram post, (5) send me an update.
```

## What you will produce

1. **Bookings sheet** – a Google Sheet with a frozen header and one row per event (date, place, items, quantity, status). Add a second tab with the hire cost for the two confirmed events using formulas (quantity × daily price), and a total.
2. **Calendar entry** – a screenshot of an event for the London call showing the time in Kenya time, a short agenda and a reminder. Add one sentence explaining how you checked the time difference.
3. **Canva post** – one social media image (a screenshot or PNG) with a headline, an offer and made-up contact details.
4. **End-of-day update** – an email-style message (100–150 words) using Done, In progress and Need from you. Include a polite holding reply (40–60 words) to the church fundraiser asking for missing details.

## Steps

1. Read the notes twice and list what is missing.
2. Build the sheet first, then check every total with a calculator.
3. Create the calendar event and the Canva post.
4. Write the update. Be short, clear and polite.
5. Collect your files and submit.

## What to submit

- Links to your Google Sheet and Canva design, and your two messages in the text box.
- Screenshots or PDF (PNG, JPG or PDF, max 5 MB) of the calendar event and the post, if you cannot share links.

## Tips

- Name files with "what + date", for example "Bookings Dec 2026".
- State assumptions. If the time zone date is unclear, say so.
- Do not promise what you cannot deliver.

```rubric
Bookings sheet and formulas | Events are organised clearly and the hire costs and total are correct using formulas | 6
Calendar entry | London call is shown at the right Kenya time with agenda and reminder, and the method is explained | 4
Canva post | Clear headline, offer and made-up contact details; readable and professional | 3
Update and holding reply | Uses Done, In progress and Need from you, is polite, within the word counts and asks for the missing details | 5
Presentation | Files open correctly, are well named and contain no real personal data | 2
```
