## Key reminders

- **Accuracy beats speed.** Check names, numbers, dates and time zones.
- **Confidentiality always.** Client files, emails and customer data stay private.
- **Confirm payment and bank-detail requests** directly with your client – fraudsters target assistants.
- **Write the time zone** on every meeting time.
- **Real clients pay you.** Never pay for "registration" or a "starter kit" to get VA work.

> **Tip:** Keep building your portfolio. Every practice task in this course – your Drive folders, receipt sheet, research summary, Trello board and Canva post – can become a sample to show clients.
