Beyond Google tools, two free apps appear in many VA job posts: **Trello** for organising tasks and **Canva** for simple designs. You do not need to be an expert – but being comfortable with both makes you more useful and helps you win more jobs.

## Trello: tasks on a board

Trello organises work as **cards** on **lists** inside a **board**. It is like sticky notes on a wall that you move from left to right as work progresses.

| Trello term | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Board | One project or client | "Baraka Hardware – Marketing" |
| List | A stage of work | To Do, Doing, Waiting, Done |
| Card | One task | "Design Easter offer poster" |
| Checklist | Small steps inside a card | Get prices ✓, draft, send for approval |
| Due date | Deadline for the card | Friday 5 pm |
| Label | Coloured tag | Urgent, Social media, Admin |
| Members | People assigned to a card | You and the client |

### A simple VA board

```text
To Do            Doing              Waiting on client     Done
---------        ---------          -----------------     ------
Weekly report    Type receipts      Approve poster        Inbox cleanup
Book venue       Supplier research                        Send invoices
```

How to use it:
1. Create a card for every task the client asks for – so nothing is forgotten.
2. Add a **due date** and a **checklist** of steps.
3. Move the card across as you work.
4. Use **comments** on the card to ask questions, so the conversation stays with the task.

> **Tip:** Some clients use similar tools such as Asana, ClickUp or Notion. The idea – tasks, stages, due dates – is the same. Learn Trello well and the others become easy.

## Canva: quick, clean designs

Canva lets you make social media posts, posters, flyers, presentations and simple documents using templates. A free account is enough to start.

### Steps to make a social media post

1. Choose a size – for example "Instagram Post (Square)".
2. Pick a template close to what you need.
3. Replace the text with the client's message.
4. Change colours and fonts to match the client's brand.
5. Replace images with the client's photos or images you are allowed to use.
6. Download as PNG (images) or PDF (for printing).

### Design rules that make you look good

- **One main message** per design. Don't fill every space.
- **Big, readable text** – people will see it on a phone screen.
- **Two fonts maximum**, and the same brand colours every time.
- **Check spelling, prices, dates and phone numbers** three times. A wrong number on 500 printed flyers is an expensive mistake.
- **Include a call to action**: "Call 07xx…", "Visit us at…", "Order on WhatsApp".

| Weak poster | Strong poster |
|---|---|
| 6 fonts, 5 colours, tiny text | 2 fonts, brand colours, big headline |
| "We sell many things" | "Easter Offer: 10% off all paint" |
| No contact details | Phone number and location clearly shown |

> **Warning:** Only use images and fonts you have the right to use. Canva's own library has licence rules – check Canva's licensing page if you are unsure. Never copy photos from another business's Facebook page.
