You can create a beautiful design and still disappoint a client by sending the wrong file: a blurry logo on a banner, a poster that prints with white edges, or a WhatsApp image that is too large to send on slow data. Knowing file formats and sizes is a mark of a professional.

## Raster vs vector

There are two kinds of images.

| | Raster (bitmap) | Vector |
|---|---|---|
| Made of | Tiny squares of colour called **pixels** | Mathematical shapes and lines |
| When enlarged | Becomes blurry or "pixelated" | Stays perfectly sharp at any size |
| Best for | Photos, social media posts | Logos, icons, illustrations, signage |
| Formats | JPG, PNG | SVG, PDF (can contain vectors), AI, EPS |

This is why a logo should ideally exist as a **vector** file: the same file can be used on a business card or on a huge billboard on Thika Road.

## The main formats

| Format | Use it for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **JPG / JPEG** | Photos, social media posts with photos | Small file size; compression reduces quality a little; no transparent background |
| **PNG** | Logos for screens, graphics with text, images needing transparent backgrounds | Sharp text; supports transparency; files can be larger |
| **SVG** | Logos and icons for websites, files for sign makers | Vector, stays sharp at any size |
| **PDF** | Printing (flyers, posters, business cards), documents to share | Keeps layout and fonts; ask the printer about their PDF settings |
| **MP4 / GIF** | Animated posts and short videos | MP4 is better quality for video |

```exercise
Q: Choose the format.
A logo that must stay sharp at any size should be saved as ___. A logo with a transparent background for a website or presentation can be a ___.
A: SVG / PDF | PNG
```

## Pixels, size and resolution

Screen images are measured in **pixels (px)**: width × height. Print is usually measured in millimetres or inches, and print quality depends on **resolution** – a common guideline for sharp printing is **300 DPI** (dots per inch) at the final print size. Always confirm the printer's requirements.

A useful example: an **A4** page is 210 × 297 mm. A5 (half of A4) is common for flyers.

## Common social media sizes

These sizes are widely used at the time of writing. Platforms change their recommendations, so check the platform's help pages or Canva's ready-made sizes.

| Use | Common size (px) | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Square post (Instagram, Facebook) | 1080 × 1080 | 1:1 |
| Portrait post (Instagram, Facebook) | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 |
| Story, Reel, TikTok, WhatsApp Status | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 |
| Profile picture | Square image, shown as a circle | Keep the logo in the centre |

```exercise
Q: Fill in the common size for a Story or WhatsApp Status.
1080 × ___ pixels
A: 1920
```

## Colour modes for export

- Screens use **RGB**. Export social media graphics in RGB.
- Commercial printers often work in **CMYK**. Ask the printer whether they want a CMYK file or will convert it themselves. Some design tools can export print-ready PDFs; check what your plan supports.

## Printing extras

- **Bleed** – extend background colours or images a few millimetres past the edge, so there are no white lines after cutting. Many printers ask for 3 mm, but confirm.
- **Crop marks** – small lines showing where to cut.
- **Fonts** – a PDF usually embeds fonts, so the printer sees your design exactly.

## Handing over files to a client

When you finish a logo, give the client an organised folder, for example:

```text
Shamba-Fresh_Logo/
  Shamba-Fresh_logo_full-colour.svg
  Shamba-Fresh_logo_full-colour.png   (transparent background)
  Shamba-Fresh_logo_white.png         (for dark backgrounds)
  Shamba-Fresh_logo_black.pdf         (one colour, for print)
  Shamba-Fresh_icon_1080.png          (profile picture)
  Shamba-Fresh_brand-guide.pdf
```

> **Tip:** Upload the final files to Google Drive and send a link. Sending many large files over WhatsApp can compress images and reduce quality – WhatsApp's "send as document" option avoids that.
