Well done – you have covered the foundations of graphic design. Before the final quiz, here is a quick review.

## What you learned

| Topic | Key idea |
|---|---|
| Principles | Contrast, alignment, hierarchy and whitespace make designs clear |
| Colour | Simple palettes (main, accent, neutrals), the 60-30-10 rule, RGB vs CMYK, readable contrast |
| Typography | Serif vs sans-serif, maximum two fonts, size and weight create hierarchy |
| Layout | Grids, proximity, rule of thirds, margins, balance |
| Canva workflow | Brief, correct size, customise templates, brand kit, check, approve, export |
| Social media posts | 3-second test, short text, mobile first, consistent template sets |
| Logos and branding | Simple, works small and in one colour, original; give a brand guide |
| File formats | JPG for photos, PNG for transparency, SVG for vectors, PDF for print |
| Business | Portfolio of best work, written agreements, deposits, honest pricing |

## Keep improving

- Design something every day, even a small post.
- Copy great designs **for practice only** to understand them – never sell or publish copies.
- Ask for honest feedback from other designers.
- Pair this course with **Digital Marketing** so you can offer clients both design and strategy.

## Practice task

Choose one real business in your area and design a mini brand pack: a logo (with a black and white version), a colour palette with HEX codes, one font pairing, two social media posts and one A5 flyer exported as a PDF. Add the best pieces to your portfolio.
