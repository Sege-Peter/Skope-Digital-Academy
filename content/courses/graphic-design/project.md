## The situation

Kiprono is opening "Kiprono's Barbershop", a made-up shop in Eldoret. He has no logo, no colours and no posts. You will design him a **mini brand pack** in Canva (free plan) or any free design app on your phone: a logo, a colour palette, two social media posts and one flyer. A fellow learner will judge the pack from your images and your short explanation.

> **Note:** Use only made-up details on your designs. Do not put a real phone number, home address or a real person's photo on them. Write "07XX XXX XXX" for the contact line and use free images or simple shapes.

## What you will produce

1. **Brand notes** – 60–100 words: who Kiprono's customers are and the feeling the brand should give (e.g. clean, friendly, strong).
2. **Logo** – one logo in colour, plus a black and white version.
3. **Palette and fonts** – main colour, accent colour and two neutrals with HEX codes, and no more than two fonts named.
4. **Two social media posts** – one offer post and one announcement post (e.g. "Now open Sundays, 9am to 6pm"), both square.
5. **One flyer** – A5 size, with a clear headline, details and a call to action.
6. **Short explanation** – 80–120 words on the design choices: contrast, hierarchy, alignment and whitespace.

## Steps

1. Re-read the lessons on principles, colour, typography and layout.
2. Write your brand notes first. Sketch about 8 quick logo ideas on paper.
3. Build your best logo, then test it small and in black and white.
4. Set your palette and fonts, then use them on every piece.
5. Design the posts and the flyer. Ask a friend to look at each for 3 seconds and tell you the main message.
6. Export the images (PNG or JPG) and the flyer (PDF).

## What to submit

- A PDF, PNG or JPG (max 5 MB) – you may combine everything into one PDF – **and/or** a Canva share link set to "anyone with the link can view".
- Your brand notes, palette and explanation in the text box.

## Tips

- Two fonts and a few colours are enough.
- Check that text is big enough to read on a phone screen.
- Credit any free image source you use.

```rubric
Brand notes and palette | Notes name the customer and feeling; palette has HEX codes and colours that suit the business | 4
Logo | Simple, readable at small size, with a working black and white version | 4
Social posts | Two posts share the same look and the main message is clear in 3 seconds | 4
Flyer | A5 layout with clear hierarchy, readable text and a call to action | 5
Explanation and presentation | The explanation uses design terms correctly, with correct spelling and no real personal data | 3
```
