**Validation** means checking that real people will actually pay for your idea *before* you spend much money on it. Friends saying "that's a nice idea" is not validation. Money, orders and repeat customers are.

## Step 1: Talk to possible customers

Speak to at least ten people who might buy. Ask about their life, not about your idea, so they do not just try to be polite.

| Weak question | Better question |
|---|---|
| "Would you buy my chapati?" | "What do you eat for lunch on site? How much do you spend?" |
| "Do you like my poster designs?" | "How did your church make its last event poster? What was hard about it?" |
| "Is this a good idea?" | "When did you last pay for something like this? Who did you pay?" |

Write down what people say. Look for the same problem coming up again and again.

## Step 2: Make a small test offer

Do the smallest thing that lets someone pay you.

- **Food:** cook 15 chapatis on one day and sell them on site.
- **Design:** make three sample posters and offer them to five churches or clubs.
- **Products:** post photos on your WhatsApp status and take orders before buying stock.

This is sometimes called a **pilot** or a **minimum viable product** – the simplest version that still solves the problem.

## Step 3: Measure what happened

```text
Test results – Fatuma's liquid soap, Mombasa
Day 1: offered to 12 neighbours, 5 bought (1 litre each)
Day 2: 3 repeat buyers, 2 new buyers
Complaints: "the smell is too weak"
Requests: "can you deliver 5 litres to my shop?"
```

Fatuma learned three things: people buy, some come back, and a shop may buy in bulk. She also learned to improve the scent.

> **Tip:** Repeat customers are the strongest sign. One person buying once may be curiosity; the same person coming back means you are solving a real problem.

## What if nobody buys?

That is useful information, and it cost you very little. Ask why: was the price wrong, the place wrong, or the product not needed? Change one thing and test again, or move to the next idea on your list.

## Signs your idea is working

- People ask "when will you have more?"
- Someone pays in advance.
- Customers tell their friends without you asking.
- You can name at least five people who bought and would buy again.

```exercise

```

## Practice task
