Once your business makes a steady profit, you will want to grow. Growth is good – but growing too fast, or in the wrong way, is one of the main reasons small businesses collapse.

## Healthy ways to grow

- **Sell more to the same customers** – add a related product (the chapati seller adds tea).
- **Reach new customers** – a new location, a delivery service, or selling online.
- **Raise your prices** when your quality and reputation justify it.
- **Reinvest profit** – put part of each month's profit back into stock or better tools.
- **Get help** – train a helper so the business does not stop when you are sick.

> **Tip:** Grow from proven demand. If customers keep asking for something you don't have, that is your next product.

## Common mistakes to avoid

| Mistake | Better habit |
|---|---|
| Eating the capital – using stock money for personal spending | Pay yourself a fixed amount; leave the rest in the business |
| No records | Write money in and money out every day |
| Too much credit to customers | Set a limit and a date; collect politely but firmly |
| Copying a business that is already crowded | Find what makes yours different |
| Taking a big loan to start | Test first; borrow only for proven needs |
| Ignoring customer complaints | Treat complaints as free advice |
| Giving up after one bad month | Look at the records, find the cause, adjust |

## Staying safe

Be alert to scams aimed at small businesses: fake "suppliers" who want full payment before delivery, fake M-Pesa confirmation messages (always check your actual balance), and people who claim to be officials asking for cash. Confirm before you pay or hand over goods.

## Course summary

Over four weeks you learned to:

1. Spot business ideas from real problems and score them.
2. Test ideas with real customers before spending much.
3. Describe a business model and understand revenue, costs and profit.
4. Start small and set a price that covers costs and leaves a profit.
5. Market on a small budget and keep customers coming back.
6. Keep simple records and follow the official steps to register.
