**Marketing** is everything you do to help the right people know about your business, trust it and buy. You do not need a big budget. You need consistency and a clear message.

## Know who you are talking to

Describe your ideal customer in one sentence:

```text
"Mothers in Kondele aged 25–45 who want their daughters' hair done
neatly and quickly before school on Monday."
```

When you know who your customer is, you know where to find them and what to say.

## Free and low-cost channels

| Channel | How to use it | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Word of mouth | Do great work and ask happy customers to tell a friend | Free |
| WhatsApp Status and groups | Post photos of real work 3–4 times a week; use a WhatsApp Business catalogue | Data only |
| Facebook, Instagram, TikTok | Short videos of the process, before/after photos, customer reviews | Data only |
| Google Business Profile | Lets people find you on Google Maps | Free |
| Signboard and flyers | A clear sign at your stall; flyers at churches, chamas, schools | Low |
| Partnerships | A salon refers clients to your nail business and you refer back | Free |

> **Tip:** Show, don't just tell. A 20-second video of you fixing a phone screen convinces more people than a long post saying "we are the best".

## Write a clear offer

A good post answers four questions: **what**, **for whom**, **how much**, **how to order**.

```text
Fresh liquid soap for homes and shops
1 litre KES 150 · 5 litres KES 650
Free delivery within Nyali on orders of 5 litres and above.
WhatsApp 07XX XXX XXX to order.
```

## Keep customers coming back

It is cheaper to keep a customer than to find a new one.

- Save customer numbers (with permission) and send occasional updates – not spam.
- Offer a loyalty reward: "Buy 9 haircuts, the 10th is free."
- Follow up: "Did the shoes fit well?"
- Handle complaints quickly and politely. An unhappy customer who is treated well often becomes loyal.

## Measure what works

Ask every new customer: "How did you hear about us?" Keep a tally for a month.

```text
WhatsApp status   ||||| |||| (9)
Friend told them  ||||| ||||| || (12)
Signboard         ||| (3)
```

This tells you to put more effort into referrals and WhatsApp, and less into the signboard.

> **Warning:** Never buy fake followers or fake reviews, and never post other people's photos as your own work. Customers notice, and it destroys trust.

```exercise
Q: A good offer answers four questions. Fill in the missing one.
What, for whom, how much, and how to ___
A: order / buy
```

```exercise
Q: In the tally, 12 came from friends, 9 from WhatsApp and 3 from the signboard. How many customers in total?
Total = ___
A: 24
```

## Practice task

Write **one clear offer post** for your business that answers what, for whom, how much and how to order. Post it on your WhatsApp Status (or show it to three friends for feedback). Start a "How did you hear about us?" tally in your notebook.
