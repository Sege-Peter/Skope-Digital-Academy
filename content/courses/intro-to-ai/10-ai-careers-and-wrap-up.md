AI is creating new kinds of work and changing old ones. You do not need a computer science degree to start – but you do need to keep learning.

## Career paths around AI

| Path | What you do | Good next steps |
|---|---|---|
| **AI-powered freelancer** | Writing, design, VA or social media work using AI tools | Build a portfolio; learn Canva, Google Workspace, prompting |
| **Data annotator / AI trainer** | Label images, text or audio; rate and correct AI answers | Strong English or Kiswahili, attention to detail; apply only via known companies |
| **Data analyst** | Clean and analyse data, make charts and reports | Spreadsheets, SQL, Python – see the Skope *Data Analytics* course |
| **AI-savvy professional** | Accountant, teacher, nurse, marketer who uses AI well | Learn the AI tools used in your field |
| **Software developer** | Build apps that use AI services | Python or JavaScript, web basics, APIs |
| **Machine learning engineer / data scientist** | Build and train models | Usually a degree or deep self-study in maths, statistics and programming |

> **Tip:** Strong maths, clear writing and good problem-solving help in every path. The Skope *Mathematics Refresher* course is a useful companion to this one.

## Course recap

- **AI** is software that performs tasks needing human-like thinking; all of today's AI is *narrow* AI.
- **Machine learning** learns patterns from examples (data and labels) instead of hand-written rules.
- **Data quality** decides model quality – garbage in, garbage out.
- **Generative AI** creates content; chatbots predict the next word (token).
- **Good prompts** use Role, Task, Context and Format, and you improve them by iterating.
- **Use AI to learn, not to skip learning**, and follow your institution's rules.
- **Earning:** AI multiplies real skills in writing, design, VA work and more – always check and personalise.
- **Limits:** hallucinations and bias are real; verify before you trust.
- **Privacy:** never paste secrets or other people's data; beware deepfake scams.
