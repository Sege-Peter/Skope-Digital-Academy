Every time you type into an AI tool, you are sending information to a company's computers. Some services may store your chats or use them to improve their models. Responsible users think before they paste.

## What happens to what you type?

Policies differ between tools and change over time, but in general:

- Your chats may be **stored** on the company's servers.
- They may be **reviewed** or **used for training** unless you switch this off (many tools have a setting for it).
- Anything you upload – photos, documents, voice notes – is data too.

Read the privacy settings of any AI tool you use regularly, and turn off chat history or training where you can if you handle sensitive material.

## Never paste these

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Secrets | Passwords, M-Pesa PIN, OTP codes, bank card numbers |
| Identity | National ID number, KRA PIN, passport scans |
| Other people's private data | Clients' phone numbers, medical details, school records |
| Confidential business data | Unreleased plans, customer lists, contracts – unless your employer approves the tool |

> **Note:** Kenya has a **Data Protection Act (2019)**, overseen by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke). If you handle other people's personal data in a job or business, you have legal responsibilities. Learn the basics from the official website.

## Safer habits

1. **Anonymise.** Replace real names and numbers with placeholders: "Customer A paid KES 3,000 late" instead of the real name and phone number.
2. **Use work-approved tools** for work data.
3. **Log out** of AI tools on shared or cyber-café computers.
4. **Use strong, unique passwords** and two-step verification on your AI and email accounts.

## Deepfakes and AI scams

AI can create fake voices, photos and videos that look real – called **deepfakes**. Criminals use them to:

- Fake a relative's voice asking for urgent money via M-Pesa
- Make fake videos of famous people "promoting" investment schemes
- Create fake job adverts and fake company profiles

**Protect yourself:** call the person back on a number you already know, never send money because of a voice note or video alone, and be suspicious of anything urgent that asks for money or codes.

## Responsible-use checklist

```text
[ ] I checked facts, numbers and sources before using the answer.
[ ] I did not paste passwords, PINs or anyone's private details.
[ ] I followed my school's or employer's AI rules.
[ ] I was honest about AI use where it matters (school work, clients).
[ ] I did not use AI to deceive, harass or copy someone else's work.
[ ] A human (me) made the final decision.
```

## Respect other people

Do not use AI to create fake images of real people, spread false news, cheat, or harass anyone. Creating or sharing such content can hurt people and can break the law. Treat AI-generated content you see online with healthy suspicion – check before you forward.
