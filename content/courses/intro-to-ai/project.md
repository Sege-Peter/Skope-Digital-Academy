## The situation

Fatuma sells second-hand clothes from a stall in Nakuru and wants help with a WhatsApp advert and a short customer reply. A free chatbot can draft both, but chatbots sometimes get facts wrong and can invent details. Your job is to use AI the way a careful freelancer would: write a clear prompt, check the answer, fix it and record what you learned. You will use any free chatbot (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot or similar).

> **Note:** Never paste real personal data into a chatbot or into your submission – no real names of customers, phone numbers, ID numbers or M-Pesa details. Use "Fatuma's Stall" and made-up details only.

## What you will produce

One document (text box, or a PDF/Word file) with five parts:

1. **Weak prompt and answer** – your first quick prompt (one line) and the chatbot's answer, pasted in.
2. **Strong prompt and answer** – a rewritten prompt that states the role, task, context and format (for example: a 60-word WhatsApp advert for Fatuma's Stall with a friendly tone and an order call to action), and the new answer.
3. **Fact-check table** – at least 5 rows: a claim from the AI answer, how you checked it (Google, a website, common sense) and whether it was correct, wrong or unclear.
4. **Final edited advert** – your improved version in 60–80 words, with every wrong or invented detail removed (you may use made-up prices and clearly mark them "example").
5. **Reflection** – 80–120 words: what changed between the weak and strong prompt, one risk of AI you noticed (hallucination, bias or privacy) and how you will handle it next time.

## Steps

1. Re-read the lessons on prompts, hallucinations and privacy.
2. Run your weak prompt, then your strong prompt, and copy both answers.
3. Underline every fact, number or promise in the answer.
4. Check each one and fill in the table.
5. Edit the advert by hand so it is accurate and sounds like a real person.
6. Write the reflection and submit.

## What to submit

- Your kit in the text box **or** as a PDF/Word file (max 5 MB), or a link to a shared chat or Google Doc (view access).

## Tips

- Tell the chatbot who the advert is for and how long it should be.
- Being honest about wrong answers earns marks; hiding them does not.
- Do not present AI text as fully your own work without saying you edited it.

```rubric
Weak versus strong prompt | The strong prompt clearly adds role, task, context and format and gives a visibly better answer | 5
Fact-check table | Five or more claims checked with a stated method and an honest result | 5
Final advert | Accurate, 60–80 words, edited by hand with no invented details left | 4
Reflection | Names a real AI risk and a practical way to handle it, 80–120 words | 4
Presentation and privacy | Clear layout, correct spelling and no real personal data | 2
```
