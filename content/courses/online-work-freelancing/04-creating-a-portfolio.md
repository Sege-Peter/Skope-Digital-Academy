A portfolio is a small collection of your best work. It proves you can do what your profile says. For a client choosing between ten beginners, the one with good samples usually wins.

## "But I have no clients yet!"

This is the most common worry, and there is a simple answer: **make your own samples**. Clients want to see the *quality* of your work. It does not have to be paid work, as long as you are honest about what it is.

| Your skill | Sample you can make this week |
|---|---|
| Writing | Two short blog posts, e.g. "5 Ways to Save Data Bundles" |
| Design | A poster for an imaginary event, a menu for a local café |
| Data entry | A neat Google Sheet listing 30 items with prices and categories |
| Virtual assistant | A sample weekly schedule and a tidy email template |
| Transcription | A typed transcript of a 3-minute public speech or talk |
| Social media | A one-week content plan for a local business, with captions |

> **Tip:** Offer to do one small job for free or cheaply for a local business, a church or a youth group – *in exchange for permission to show the work and a short written review*. This gives you a real sample and a real reference.

## Rules for good samples

1. **Quality over quantity.** Three excellent samples beat fifteen average ones.
2. **Match your offer.** If you sell posters, show posters – not your poems.
3. **Label practice work honestly.** For example: "Sample project – menu design for an imaginary café".
4. **Respect other people's work.** Never show someone else's design or writing as yours. Use your own photos, free images you are allowed to use, or simple shapes and text.
5. **Protect private information.** Remove client names, phone numbers and prices if the client has not given you permission to share them.

## Where to keep your portfolio

You do not need to build a website on day one.

- **Platform portfolio sections** – Upwork, Fiverr and LinkedIn all let you upload samples.
- **Google Drive** – put samples in a folder and share a "view only" link.
- **Canva** – design a simple 3–5 page portfolio and share it as a link or PDF.
- **Behance** (for designers) or a free blog (for writers).

When sharing a Google Drive link, check the sharing setting is "Anyone with the link – Viewer", and open it in a private browser window to make sure it works before you send it.

## Describe each sample

Under every sample, write two or three lines:

```text
Menu design – lakeside fish café in Kisumu (practice project)
Goal: a clear one-page menu that is easy to read on WhatsApp.
Tools: Canva. Time taken: 2 hours.
```

This short note shows the client how you think: you understood a goal, chose a tool and worked efficiently.

## Kiprono's portfolio

Kiprono wants data entry work. He creates a Google Sheet with 50 made-up products for a hardware shop: name, category, price in KES and stock level. He adds filters, freezes the header row and uses colours for low stock. He takes a screenshot and adds it to his Upwork portfolio with the note "Practice project – inventory sheet for a hardware shop". When he applies for data entry jobs, he links to it. Clients can see exactly how tidy his work is.
