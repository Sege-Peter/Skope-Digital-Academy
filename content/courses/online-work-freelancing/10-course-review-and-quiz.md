## Key reminders

- Your first client is the hardest. Keep improving your profile, samples and proposals rather than giving up.
- Keep all platform work and payments **on the platform**.
- **Real clients pay you.** Anyone asking you to pay to start work is a red flag.
- Never share passwords, PINs or OTPs.
- Check fees, payment methods and tax rules on **official websites** (the platform's help pages, safaricom.co.ke, kra.go.ke) because they change.
- Reply on time, deliver on time, and ask for a review when the client is happy.

> **Tip:** Set a 30-day goal now – for example "send 20 good proposals and finish 2 local jobs". Small, steady targets beat big dreams with no plan.
