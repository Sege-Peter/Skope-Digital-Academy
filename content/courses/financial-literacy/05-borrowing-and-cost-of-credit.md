**Credit** means using someone else's money now and paying it back later, usually with an extra cost. Borrowing can help – for example, buying equipment that earns income. It can also trap you if you borrow for wants or cannot repay.

## Where young Kenyans borrow

| Source | Speed | Things to watch |
|---|---|---|
| Family and friends | Fast | Relationships can suffer; agree terms clearly |
| Chama or SACCO | Medium | Usually need to save first; guarantors may be needed |
| Bank or microfinance | Slower | Paperwork, sometimes security (collateral) |
| **Digital (mobile) loans** | Instant | Short repayment periods; costs can be high; late payment may lead to negative credit reports |
| Shopkeeper credit ("deni") | Instant | Easy to lose track of |
| Buy-now-pay-later phones and items | Fast | The total price is often much higher than paying cash |

## The true cost of a loan

Lenders may describe costs in different ways – interest, "facilitation fee", "processing fee", insurance, excise duty, late penalties. To compare loans, always find the **total amount you will repay**.

```text
True cost = Total amount you repay − Amount you actually received
```

**Example (illustrative numbers, not a real lender's terms)**

| | Loan A (app) | Loan B (SACCO) |
|---|---|---|
| Amount received | KES 5,000 | KES 5,000 |
| Repayment period | 1 month | 6 months |
| Total repaid | KES 5,500 | KES 5,450 |
| True cost | KES 500 | KES 450 |

At first both look similar. But Loan A costs KES 500 for **one month**, while Loan B costs KES 450 for **six months**. If you kept renewing a loan like Loan A every month for six months, the cost would be about 6 × 500 = KES 3,000. The length of time matters a lot.

> **Note:** Interest rates and fee rules for lenders in Kenya change, and some digital lenders must be licensed by the Central Bank of Kenya. Before borrowing, check the lender's licence and guidance on [centralbank.go.ke](https://www.centralbank.go.ke), and ask for the full cost in writing.

## Credit reports

When you borrow, lenders may share your repayment record with **credit reference bureaus (CRBs)**. Paying on time builds a good record. Paying late or defaulting can make it hard to get a loan later – sometimes even a job or a house. You can request your own credit report; check the official process before paying anyone who offers to "clear" your name.

## Dangers of digital loans

- They are **too easy** – a few taps and the money is there, so people borrow for wants.
- **Short periods** – if your income is irregular, repayment comes before you have the money.
- **Loan stacking** – borrowing from one app to pay another. The total cost multiplies quickly.
- **Harassment and privacy** – some illegal lenders contact your friends and family. Use only licensed lenders.

## Before you borrow, ask

1. Is this a need, or a want I could save for?
2. Will it help me earn more than it costs?
3. What is the **total** I will repay, and when?
4. Can I repay even if my income drops next month?
5. Have I compared at least two options?

If you answer "no" or "I don't know" to any of these, wait.

```exercise
Q: You receive KES 3,000 and repay KES 3,360. What is the true cost of the loan?
True cost = KES ___
A: 360
```

```exercise
Q: A loan costs KES 400 per month and you keep renewing it for 5 months. What is the total cost?
Total cost = KES ___
A: 2000 / 2,000
```
