The documents customers ask for most often are **letters** and **CVs**. Both depend on clean layout, and tables help a lot. Let us build them.

## Inserting a table

1. Click where the table should go.
2. Go to **Insert** › **Table** and drag across the grid to choose rows and columns (e.g. 3 × 4).
3. Type in the cells. Press **Tab** to move to the next cell; pressing **Tab** in the last cell adds a new row.

When you click inside a table, extra tabs appear (named **Table Design** and **Layout**, or **Table Tools** in older versions). Use them to:

- Add or delete rows and columns.
- **Merge cells** (join several cells into one, e.g. for a title row).
- Apply a ready-made table style.
- Remove borders – useful for invisible layout tables in CVs.

## Writing a formal letter

Kenyan offices generally use the **block format**: everything starts at the left margin, with a blank line between parts.

```text
Brian Kiprono
P.O. Box 1234-30100
Eldoret
0712 345 678
brian.kiprono@example.com

12 March 2026

The Human Resources Manager
Rift Valley Hardware Ltd
P.O. Box 5678-30100
Eldoret

Dear Sir/Madam,

RE: APPLICATION FOR THE POSITION OF SHOP ASSISTANT

I am writing to apply for the position of Shop Assistant advertised on your
notice board on 10 March 2026. I recently completed my KCSE and I am
comfortable using computers, handling customers and keeping simple records.

During the school holidays I helped at my aunt's shop in Kapsabet, where I
served customers, recorded daily sales in a notebook and received M-Pesa
payments.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how I can support your team. I am
available for an interview at any time.

Yours faithfully,

(signature)

Brian Kiprono
```

> **Note:** Use **Yours faithfully** when you open with *Dear Sir/Madam*, and **Yours sincerely** when you open with the person's name (*Dear Ms Wanjiru*).

The **RE:** line (the subject) is usually bold, and often underlined or in capitals.

## Building a simple CV

A good beginner CV is one or two pages and has these sections:

| Section | What to include |
|---|---|
| Contact details | Name, phone, email, town (no need for ID number or a photo unless asked) |
| Profile | 2–3 sentences about who you are and what you offer |
| Education | Most recent first: school, years, KCSE grade |
| Skills | Computer packages, languages, customer service, M-Pesa handling |
| Experience | Jobs, volunteering, family business, attachment |
| Referees | Two or three people, with permission, or "Available on request" |

### CV formatting tips

- Use **Heading 2** for section names so they look identical.
- Use a **borderless two-column table** to line up dates on the left and details on the right.
- Stick to one clean font (Calibri, Arial) at 10.5–12 pt.
- Save the final copy as **PDF** (**File** › **Save As** or **Export**, then choose PDF).

> **Warning:** Never invent qualifications or employers on a CV – yours or a customer's. Employers check, and fake documents can lead to dismissal or criminal charges.
