## Pricing your work

Charges vary a lot by town and by job, so research what cyber cafés near you charge before you set prices. Common ways to price:

- **Per page** for typing (handwritten work costs more than typed corrections).
- **Per document** for CVs and letters.
- **Per job** for spreadsheets, presentations and report formatting – agree the price before you start.

> **Note:** Any figures you hear from other typists are only examples. Your price depends on your speed, quality and location. Start fair, build reviews, then raise prices as your work improves.

## Working professionally

1. **Confirm the details** – spelling of names, phone numbers, dates. Read them back to the customer.
2. **Proofread** – run spell check, then read the whole document slowly yourself.
3. **Show a draft** before printing many copies.
4. **Deliver as PDF** (and Word if the customer will edit it).
5. **Protect privacy** – delete customers' files from shared computers and never post their documents online.
6. **Get paid properly** – use your own M-Pesa till or number, and send the final file after payment for new customers.

> **Warning:** Be careful of online "typing jobs" that ask you to pay a registration or "activation" fee before you get work. Genuine clients pay you; you do not pay them to work.

## Improve your typing speed

Fast, accurate typing is your main tool. Practise 15 minutes a day on a free typing-practice website, keeping your fingers on the home row (**A S D F** and **J K L ;**). Accuracy first – speed follows.
