## The situation

Kiprono has opened a small typing and printing desk in Nakuru. His customers need letters, price lists and simple reports done well. He wants you to produce a sample pack he can show new customers. You will make a Word document, an Excel sheet and a short slide show, all about a made-up business called "Kiprono Digital Desk".

> **Note:** Use made-up names, phone numbers and email addresses. Do not put your own ID number, real phone number or anyone's private details in your files.

## What you will produce

Three files (Microsoft Office or the free Google Docs, Sheets and Slides):

1. **Word or Docs document** – a one-page price list and services sheet for Kiprono Digital Desk. It must have a title, headings in a proper style, a table with at least six services, and a made-up contact line. Use any example figures you like, but label them as examples.
2. **Excel or Sheets file** – a sheet of one week's sales with at least eight rows: date, service, quantity, price and a *Value* column using a formula. Add totals, one IF or SUMIF formula, and one chart with a title and axis labels.
3. **Slides** – exactly 5 slides: title, what we do, our price list, why choose us, contact. Use short bullet points and the same colours on every slide.

## Steps

1. Create a folder with a clear name and save your three files in it.
2. Make the Word document. Use heading styles, not just bigger text.
3. Build the spreadsheet with formulas, not typed totals. Check the totals by hand.
4. Make the slides. Keep each slide to a few short lines.
5. Open all three files again and fix anything that looks messy.

## What to submit

- Share links to the three files (Google Drive, viewable by anyone with the link) in the text box, **or** upload one PDF that contains all three (max 5 MB).
- If you used Word, Excel and PowerPoint, export them to PDF first.

## Tips

- If a reviewer cannot open your link, they cannot mark it. Test it in a private window.
- One font, two colours and lots of white space looks professional.
- Click on a total cell to show that it is a formula.
