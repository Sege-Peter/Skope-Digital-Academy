## Key reminders

- Every customer deserves the same respect.
- Listen first; confirm you understood before acting.
- Be specific: real times, real next steps, promises you can keep.
- Good service never means accepting abuse – involve your supervisor.
- Never share or ask for PINs or OTPs, and don't trust payment screenshots.
- Real employers don't ask you to pay to get a job.

> **Tip:** Customer service skills help in every career – sales, nursing, teaching, banking, hospitality and running your own business. Keep practising them every day.
