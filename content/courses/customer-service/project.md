## The situation

Fatuma owns "Fatuma's Fashion Corner", a small clothes and shoes shop in Kakamega. She sells in person and takes orders on WhatsApp. Lately customers have complained about slow replies and a wrong order. She asks you to build a short customer service toolkit for her and her one employee. The shop and all the people are made up.

> **Note:** Use made-up customer names and phone numbers. Do not include real customers' messages, numbers or photos. If you test your feedback form, do not collect anyone's phone number or ID.

Here is the complaint you must answer:

```text
WhatsApp message from customer "Brian":
"I paid KES 1,500 by M-Pesa for the shoes on Monday. It is Friday and
nothing has arrived. Nobody replies to me. This is very poor service."
```

## What you will produce

One document with five parts:

1. **WhatsApp quick replies** – three short replies: a greeting, opening hours and location (made up), and how to pay. Each under 40 words.
2. **Reply to Brian** – a message of 80 to 130 words that uses the LEARN steps from the course: listen, empathise, apologise once, resolve with specific next steps, and note what to do next time.
3. **Difficult customer script** – pick one customer type from the course. Write what the customer says and your calm reply (2 to 4 lines each).
4. **Feedback form** – five questions you would ask customers (a mix of rating and open questions). Add a link to a Google Form if you made one.
5. **Three improvements** – three low-cost changes for the shop, each with one sentence on why it helps.

## Steps

1. Reread your notes on phone and WhatsApp etiquette and on handling complaints.
2. Write the quick replies and read each as if you were the customer.
3. Draft the reply to Brian. Do not blame him or make excuses.
4. Write the script, the five feedback questions and the three improvements.
5. Proofread everything.

## What to submit

- Your toolkit in the text box **or** as a PDF/Word file (max 5 MB).
- Optional: a link to your Google Form (set to view-only for testing).

## Tips

- Say sorry once and then move to what you will do.
- Do not promise what the shop cannot deliver, such as a refund date you invented.
- Keep your tone warm, not copied from a robot.
