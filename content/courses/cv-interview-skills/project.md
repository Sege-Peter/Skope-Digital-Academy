## The situation

Achieng has just finished Form Four in Kisumu and has found a job post for a shop assistant. She has little work experience but plenty of school, church and family responsibilities to show. In this project you will build an application pack for the made-up job below: a CV, a cover letter and two interview answers. You can write about yourself or about a made-up person like Achieng.

> **Note:** Use made-up contact details. Write a made-up phone number (for example 07XX XXX XXX) and a made-up email. Do not put your ID number, home address, date of birth, passwords or real referee details in your submission. Real people reviewing your work should not be able to contact you.

```text
Job: Shop Assistant, Lakeview Mini-Mart, Kisumu
We need a friendly, honest person to serve customers, arrange stock, handle
cash and M-Pesa payments, and keep the shop clean. No experience needed.
Apply with a CV and a short cover letter.
```

## What you will produce

One document with four parts:

1. **CV** – one page with a heading, a profile of 3 sentences, education, experience or activities (at least four bullets, each starting with an action verb and at least two containing a number), skills, and "Referees available on request".
2. **Cover letter** – 150 to 220 words in four parts: opening, why you fit, one example, closing.
3. **Two STAR answers** – for "Tell me about a time you solved a problem" and "Tell me about a time you worked in a team". Each answer is 80 to 120 words with the four STAR parts.
4. **Scam check** – three sentences on how you would check that a job post like this is genuine before applying.

## Steps

1. Reread the lessons on CV structure and achievements.
2. List what you have done in the last three years and choose your best four activities.
3. Write the CV and tailor the profile to the shop job.
4. Write the cover letter. Use the shop's needs, not just your own.
5. Write the STAR answers and the scam check.
6. Read everything aloud, fix mistakes, then submit.

## What to submit

- Your pack in the text box **or** as a PDF or Word file (max 5 MB).
- Optional: a link to a Google Docs version.

## Tips

- Use numbers where you can, for example "served about 30 customers a day".
- Never pay money to apply for a job.
- Do not invent experience. Use real activities, but keep all contact details made up.

```rubric
CV | CV fits one page, has the right sections and at least four action-verb bullets with some numbers | 6
Cover letter | Letter is 150-220 words, targets the Lakeview job and gives one concrete example | 5
STAR answers | Two answers each show situation, task, action and result in 80-120 words | 5
Scam check | Three sentences show sensible ways to check a job is genuine | 2
Privacy and presentation | Spelling is correct and only made-up contact details are used | 2
```
