The difference between a weak CV and a strong one is usually not *what* the person did, but *how they describe it*. In this lesson you will turn your everyday activities into convincing CV points.

## Duties versus achievements

A **duty** is what you were supposed to do. An **achievement** is what happened because you did it well.

| Weak (duty) | Strong (achievement) |
|---|---|
| Worked in my mother's shop | Served about 40 customers a day in my family's shop and kept the daily sales book |
| Was a games captain | Organised weekly training for 25 students as games captain; our team reached the county finals |
| Did a computer course | Completed a 6-week computer course; can now create spreadsheets and format documents |

The strong versions use **action verbs**, **numbers** and **results**.

## Start with an action verb

Begin each point with a strong verb instead of "I was responsible for".

Useful action verbs: *served, organised, managed, sold, trained, recorded, designed, repaired, led, collected, improved, created, taught, planned.*

## Add numbers

Numbers make your points believable and easy to picture: how many customers, how much money, how many people, how many weeks. Use honest estimates ("about", "up to") if you do not know the exact figure.

```text
- Sold second-hand clothes at Gikomba market on Saturdays,
  earning up to KES 3,000 profit per month.
- Collected and recorded monthly contributions for a 15-member chama
  using a simple Excel sheet.
- Tutored three Form One students in Mathematics; all improved
  their end-of-term marks.
```

## A sample experience section

```text
EXPERIENCE
Shop Assistant (family business) — Mama Fatuma Grocery, Mombasa
Jan 2024 – Present (holidays and weekends)
- Served customers and handled cash and M-Pesa payments.
- Restocked shelves and told the owner when stock was running low.
- Kept a daily record of sales in an exercise book.

Volunteer — Kisauni Youth Clean-Up Group
2023 – 2025
- Helped organise monthly clean-ups with 30+ volunteers.
- Designed posters on Canva to advertise events on WhatsApp.
```

## The skills section

Split skills into groups so they are easy to read.

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| Computer | Microsoft Word, Excel (basic formulas), Google Docs, Canva, email |
| Languages | English (fluent), Kiswahili (fluent), Dholuo (spoken) |
| People | Customer service, handling complaints politely, teamwork |
| Practical | Cash handling, stock keeping, driving (if you have a licence) |

> **Warning:** Only list skills you can prove in an interview or a test. If you write "Excel – advanced", expect to be asked to build a spreadsheet on the spot.

## Matching the job advert

Read the advert carefully and underline the skills it asks for. Then make sure those words appear in your CV (if they are true for you). Many large companies use software that scans CVs for these keywords before a person ever sees them.

```exercise
Q: Rewrite these weak words into strong CV language by filling the blanks.
"I was responsible for customers" becomes "___ about 40 customers a day".
Using ___ like "40 customers" makes a CV point more believable.
A: Served / Assisted / Helped | numbers / figures
H: Start with an action verb. What did you add to the sentence that makes it measurable?
```

## Practice task

Take your activity list from Lesson 1. Pick your best four activities and write two strong bullet points for each, starting with an action verb and including at least one number. Add them to the Experience and Achievements sections of your CV draft.
