A **cover letter** (or application letter) is a short letter that goes with your CV. Your CV lists facts; the cover letter explains **why you want this particular job** and **why you are a good fit**. Many Kenyan employers still ask for one, especially for internships, attachments and government jobs.

## When you need one

- When the advert asks for "an application letter and CV".
- When you email your CV to a company: the email itself can act as a short cover letter.
- When you drop a printed CV at an office: attach a one-page letter.

## The structure

Keep it to **one page**, about 200–300 words, in four short parts.

| Part | What to say |
|---|---|
| Opening | Which job you are applying for and where you saw it |
| Why you fit | 2–3 points linking your skills or experience to the job |
| Why them | One sentence showing you know something about the company |
| Closing | Thank them, say you are available for interview, give your phone number |

## A sample cover letter

```text
Kiprono Kipchumba
P.O. Box 123, Eldoret
0711 000 000 | kiprono.kipchumba@gmail.com

12 February 2026

The Branch Manager
Highland Hardware Ltd
Eldoret

Dear Sir/Madam,

RE: APPLICATION FOR THE POSITION OF SALES ASSISTANT

I am writing to apply for the Sales Assistant position advertised on
your Facebook page on 10 February 2026.

I completed my KCSE in 2025 and have spent the last two years helping
in my uncle's agrovet shop during school holidays. There I served
farmers, explained products, handled M-Pesa payments and helped with
stock counts. I enjoy talking to customers and I am comfortable with
basic Excel.

I admire how Highland Hardware supports local builders with credit
and delivery, and I would be glad to help serve your customers.

My CV is attached. I am available for an interview at any time and can
be reached on 0711 000 000. Thank you for considering my application.

Yours faithfully,
Kiprono Kipchumba
```

> **Note:** Use "Yours faithfully" when you started with "Dear Sir/Madam". Use "Yours sincerely" when you wrote to a named person, such as "Dear Ms. Wanjiru".

## Tips that make a difference

- **Name the job exactly** as it appears in the advert, and include any reference number.
- **Do not repeat your whole CV.** Pick the two or three points that matter most for this job.
- **Be specific about the company.** Look at their website or social media pages for one real detail.
- **Sound confident, not desperate.** Avoid lines like "I am very needy and any job will do."
- **Proofread.** A spelling mistake in a one-page letter is very noticeable.

## Emailing an application

When you apply by email, write a clear subject line and a short message, then attach both files as PDFs.

```text
Subject: Application for Sales Assistant – Kiprono Kipchumba

Dear Hiring Manager,

Please find attached my application letter and CV for the Sales
Assistant position advertised on your Facebook page.

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,
Kiprono Kipchumba
0711 000 000
```

## Practice task

Find a real entry-level job advert (on a company's official page, a newspaper or a trusted job site). Write a cover letter for it using the four-part structure. Read it aloud to check it sounds like you, then save it as a PDF.
