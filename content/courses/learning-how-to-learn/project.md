## The situation

Achieng has finished school and wants to learn one new skill – Canva design – using free videos on her phone. She keeps starting and stopping. Her problem is not talent, it is **method**: she re-reads, she scrolls instead of studying, and she has no plan. In this project you will do what she should do. You will choose one skill you really want, build a learning plan, **follow it for a few days** and report honestly on what happened.

> **Note:** Use made-up or general details. Do **not** include your phone number, ID number, school admission number or home address. A first name is enough.

## What you will produce

One document with four parts:

1. **Your skill and your why** – 60–100 words: the one skill you will learn, why it matters to you, and a goal with a date (e.g. "make three posters by 14 December").
2. **Your two-week plan** – a table with at least 8 study sessions (date, time, 20–40 minutes, what you will study, which method you will use). Use at least three methods from the course: active recall, spaced repetition, Pomodoro, Cornell notes.
3. **Evidence of practice** – ten flashcards (question and answer) or one page of Cornell notes from a real 10–15 minute lesson, and your review dates for those cards.
4. **Study log and reflection** – a log of at least 4 sessions you really completed (date, minutes, what you recalled without looking, a score such as 6 out of 10). Then write 100–150 words: what worked, what did not, and one change for next week.

## Steps

1. Pick a skill you can start today with free resources (YouTube, a free course, a library book).
2. Write part 1, then fill in your table in a notebook, spreadsheet or Google Doc.
3. Watch or read one short lesson and make your flashcards or Cornell notes.
4. Do your first sessions. Test yourself **before** checking the answers.
5. Fill in the log straight after each session, not at the end.
6. Write the reflection, check spelling and submit.

## What to submit

- Your plan, flashcards/notes and log in the text box **or** as a PDF, Word file or clear photo/scan (PNG or JPG) – max 5 MB.
- Optional: a link to a Google Sheet or Doc with your plan.

## Tips

- Be honest. A log with two missed sessions and a clear lesson learned scores better than a perfect-looking one.
- Make flashcards in your own words, one idea per card.
- Put your sessions in your phone calendar with reminders.
- Never pay anyone for a "guaranteed" course in this project – free resources only.
