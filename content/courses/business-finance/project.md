## The situation

Brian wants to start selling fried chips and sausages from a cart near a stage in Eldoret. He has some savings but has never planned the money side. He asks you to build a simple one-month money plan so he knows whether the idea can work. Use the made-up figures below. You may change them, but say clearly that you did.

> **Note:** Use made-up numbers only. Do not share your own bank, SACCO or M-Pesa statements, PINs or ID numbers.

```text
Selling price of one portion: KES 100
Variable cost of one portion (potatoes, oil, sausage, paper): KES 55
Fixed costs per month: cart rent KES 3,000, licence set-aside KES 1,000,
phone and airtime KES 500
Brian's savings to start: KES 8,000
Brian wants to pay himself KES 6,000 a month
Expected sales: 6 days a week, about 40 portions a day
```

## What you will produce

One document with five parts:

1. **Costs table** – fixed and variable costs, with totals.
2. **Break-even** – the contribution per portion and the number of portions needed to break even in a month, with your working shown.
3. **Month budget** – expected sales, variable costs, fixed costs, profit and Brian's pay.
4. **Four-week cash flow** – a table with opening cash, cash in, cash out and closing cash for each week. Mark any week with low cash.
5. **Advice** – 80 to 150 words: is the plan realistic, what are the risks, and one change you suggest.

## Steps

1. List and total the costs, then work out the contribution per portion.
2. Calculate break-even and compare it with the expected sales.
3. Fill the budget, then spread it over four weeks for the cash flow.
4. Check that each week's opening cash equals the last week's closing cash.
5. Write your advice, then proofread.

## What to submit

- A Google Sheets or Docs link (viewable by anyone with the link) **or** a PDF, photo or Word file (max 5 MB).
- Use the text box for your advice and notes.

## Tips

- Show the sum next to every answer so a reviewer can follow it.
- Do not mix Brian's personal money with the business money.
- A plan that says "this will not work yet, here is what to change" can still score full marks.

```rubric
Costs and break-even | Costs are sorted correctly into fixed and variable and the break-even working is correct and shown | 5
Month budget | Budget includes sales, both cost types, profit and Brian's pay with correct arithmetic | 4
Cash flow | Four weeks are laid out with correct opening and closing cash and low weeks are marked | 5
Advice | Advice is honest, 80-150 words, names a risk and suggests a practical change | 4
Presentation | Tables are tidy, labelled with KES and easy for a beginner to read | 2
```
