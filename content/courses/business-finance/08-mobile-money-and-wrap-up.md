In Kenya, most small businesses receive and send money by mobile money. Used well, it makes record keeping easier and reduces the risks of carrying cash. Used carelessly, it mixes personal and business money and exposes you to fraud.

## Mobile money options for businesses

| Option | What it is | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Personal M-Pesa line used only for business | A second SIM kept for business | Very small start-ups |
| **Pochi la Biashara** | Lets customers pay you as a business while you use your own line | Small traders and online sellers |
| **Buy Goods (Till number)** | A business till that customers pay into | Shops, kiosks, salons |
| **Paybill** | A business number with account references | Businesses with many customers or invoices (schools, landlords, suppliers) |

Each option has its own registration requirements and transaction charges, which change from time to time. Check the current details on the provider's official website or at an official shop before choosing.

## Benefits

- **Automatic records** – your statement shows every payment with date, time and sender.
- **Less cash handling** – less risk of theft or giving wrong change.
- **Separation** – a till or business line keeps business money apart from personal money.
- **Proof for lenders** – a steady statement can support a loan application with some lenders.

## Using your statement as a record

Download your business statement every week or month and compare it with your sales book. This check is called **reconciliation**.

```text
Week summary – Achieng's salon (Till)
Sales book total (M-Pesa sales):   KES 14,200
Till statement total received:     KES 13,700
Difference:                        KES 500  → one customer said she paid
                                              but the payment never arrived
```

Without the check, Achieng would never have noticed the missing KES 500.

## Staying safe

> **Warning:** Never release goods because of an SMS alone. Fake "you have received" messages are common. Confirm in your actual M-Pesa balance or till statement.

- Never share your PIN, even with staff or "customer care" callers. Real customer care will never ask for it.
- Be careful with "wrong number" requests to send money back – check your balance and use the provider's official reversal process.
- Give helpers limited access, and review the statement daily.
- Lock your phone and the M-Pesa app with a strong PIN.
