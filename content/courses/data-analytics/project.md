## The situation

Kiprono owns three small grocery shops in Kisumu, Nakuru and Eldoret. He wants to know which branch and which product bring in the most money, and where to focus next month. You have been given a messy sales list (made up for this project). Your job is to clean it, analyse it in a spreadsheet and present the findings in a short story.

> **Note:** The data below is made up. If you create extra data, do not include real customer names, phone numbers or M-Pesa details.

```text
date, branch, product, qty, amount_kes
01/04, Kisumu, Bread, 10, 650
01/04, nakuru, Milk, 20, 1200
02/04, Kisumu , Sugar, 5, 800
02/04, Eldoret, Bread, 8, KES 520
03/04, Nakuru, Bread, 12, 780
03/04, Kisumu, Milk, 15, 900
04/04, Eldoret, Sugar, 4, 640
04/04, Nakuru, Sugar, 6, 960
05/04, Eldoret, Milk, 9, 540
05/04, Kisumu, Bread, 14, 910
06/04, Nakuru, Milk, , 700
06/04, Eldoret, Bread, 11, 715
```

## What you will produce

1. **Cleaned table** – copy the data into Google Sheets or Excel and fix the spelling and spacing of branch names, remove "KES" from amounts, and mark the missing quantity. Add a short list of every change you made.
2. **Calculations** – use formulas to find the total sales, average sale, number of sales per branch (COUNTIF) and total per branch (SUMIF).
3. **Pivot table** – total amount by branch and product.
4. **Chart and story** – one chart with a title that states the finding and labelled axes, plus a three-line story: **What**, **Why**, **Now what**.

## Steps

1. Copy the data into a sheet with one value per cell.
2. Clean it and write down each change.
3. Add your formulas and check one or two by hand.
4. Make the pivot table and the chart.
5. Write the story for Kiprono in plain words.

## What to submit

- A Google Sheets link (viewable by anyone with the link) **or** a PDF, PNG or JPG of your work (max 5 MB).
- The text box can hold your change list and story.

## Tips

- Never delete data silently. Say what you changed and why.
- Choose a chart that suits the question, such as a bar chart for comparing branches.
- Be honest about missing data. Do not guess a number without saying so.

```rubric
Data cleaning | Branch names and amounts are fixed, the missing value is flagged and every change is listed | 5
Formulas | Total, average and per-branch counts and sums use correct formulas with correct results | 5
Pivot table | The pivot table shows total amount by branch and product and is easy to read | 4
Chart and story | Chart has a finding-style title and labelled axes, and the What, Why, Now what story matches the data | 5
Clarity | Sheet is tidy and a beginner can follow it | 1
```
