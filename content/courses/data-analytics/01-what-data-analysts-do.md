Every business collects numbers: sales, stock, customers, M-Pesa payments, website visits. Most of it just sits there. A **data analyst** turns that raw data into answers that help people make better decisions.

## A day in the life

Imagine Achieng works as a junior data analyst for a supermarket chain with branches in Kisumu, Nakuru and Eldoret. A manager asks: *"Why did sales drop last month in Nakuru?"*

Achieng:

1. **Asks questions** to understand the problem – which products? Compared to which month?
2. **Collects data** – exports sales records from the till system into a spreadsheet.
3. **Cleans the data** – fixes misspelt product names, removes duplicate rows, fills gaps.
4. **Analyses** – compares sales by product, week and branch.
5. **Visualises** – makes a simple chart showing cooking-oil sales fell sharply in week 3.
6. **Shares the story** – "Cooking-oil stock ran out for 9 days in week 3. Restocking faster could recover about KES 200,000 a month."

That final sentence is what the manager needed. The analyst's value is not the spreadsheet – it is the **insight** and the **recommendation**.

## The data analysis process

| Step | Key question |
|---|---|
| **Ask** | What decision are we trying to make? |
| **Collect** | What data do we need, and where is it? |
| **Clean** | Is the data correct, complete and consistent? |
| **Analyse** | What patterns, trends or differences do we see? |
| **Visualise** | What chart shows this most clearly? |
| **Share** | What should the decision-maker do now? |

## Who hires data analysts?

Almost every sector: banks and SACCOs, telcos, supermarkets, hospitals, NGOs running health or farming projects, county governments, betting and e-commerce companies, schools and universities. Many small businesses also need occasional help – a chance for freelancers.

## Tools of the trade

| Tool | Used for | In this course |
|---|---|---|
| **Spreadsheets** (Excel, Google Sheets) | Cleaning, formulas, pivot tables, charts | Yes – the main tool |
| **SQL** | Getting data out of databases | Yes – an introduction |
| **Python** | Automating analysis on bigger data | Yes – an introduction |
| **Power BI / Looker Studio / Tableau** | Interactive dashboards | Mentioned – learn later |

> **Tip:** You can start today with free Google Sheets on a phone or a cyber-café computer. Expensive software is not required to learn the skills.

## Skills that matter beyond tools

- **Curiosity** – asking "why?" more than once
- **Attention to detail** – one wrong decimal point can change a decision
- **Basic maths** – percentages, averages, comparing numbers
- **Communication** – explaining findings in plain language to non-technical people
- **Honesty** – reporting what the data shows, even when it is not what the boss hoped
