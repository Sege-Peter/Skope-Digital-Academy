## The situation

Achieng sells handmade beaded jewellery from a small stall in Kisumu. Most of her customers find her on WhatsApp, but some ask: "Do you have a website?" She cannot afford a big agency. In this project you will design and publish a **one-page website** for her business – or for any **made-up local business** of your own – that works on a phone and makes it easy to contact her.

> **Note:** Use made-up details for contact information, such as a fake phone number like 07XX XXX XXX and an email like hello@example.com. Do **not** include real phone numbers, ID numbers or addresses of anyone. Use only photos you took, free-licence photos or simple colour blocks.

## What you will produce

A published one-page site with these parts:

1. **Plan** (text box, 80–120 words): the business, its main goal (for example "get WhatsApp orders"), the target customer and the colours and fonts you chose.
2. **Page structure** – a `header` with the business name and menu, a hero section with a clear call-to-action button, at least three `section` blocks (for example products, about, contact) and a `footer`.
3. **Styling** – a colour palette in CSS variables, two fonts, a Flexbox row of at least three product or service cards that wraps on small screens, and text colours with good contrast.
4. **Responsive and accessible** – works on a phone-width screen with no sideways scrolling, images with meaningful `alt` text, and a small contact form with at least four labelled fields and one `required` field.
5. **Published link** – the site live on a free static host such as GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages, with a page `<title>` and a meta description.

## Steps

1. Write your plan and sketch the phone layout on paper.
2. Build the HTML structure, then add CSS.
3. Resize the browser window to check phone, tablet and laptop widths.
4. Run a free contrast checker on your main text and button.
5. Publish, open the link on your phone, and ask a friend what is confusing.
6. Paste your code and links for the reviewers.

## What to submit

- The **live link** to your site, and a link to your code (GitHub, CodePen or similar) **or** your full HTML and CSS pasted in the text box.
- Your plan in the text box.
- Optional: a phone screenshot (PNG or JPG, max 5 MB).

## Tips

- Test your link in a private browser tab before submitting so peers can open it.
- Keep it simple: fewer sections, done well.
- Compress images before uploading so the page loads fast on mobile data.
