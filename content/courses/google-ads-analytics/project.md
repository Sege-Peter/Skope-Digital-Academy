## The situation

Fatuma runs "Fatuma's Cakes", a made-up home bakery in Thika that sells birthday cakes, cupcakes and wedding cakes, with delivery in town. She has KES 9,000 a month she could spend on Google search ads and asks you to plan the campaign before she spends anything. You will not need a real Google Ads account: this is a **paper plan** that a peer can check.

> **Note:** Use made-up details only. Do not include real phone numbers, personal addresses, passwords or anyone's account access. If you invent a phone number for the ad, write "07XX XXX XXX".

## What you will produce

One document (text box, or a PDF/Word file) with five parts:

1. **Goal and location** – one sentence each: what Fatuma wants (e.g. more cake orders on WhatsApp) and where the ads will show.
2. **Account structure** – one campaign with 2–3 ad groups. For each ad group list at least 4 keywords and mark the match type you would use.
3. **Negative keywords** – 5 words or phrases to exclude, each with a short reason.
4. **Ad copy** – for one ad group, 4 headlines (30 characters or fewer each, count them) and 2 descriptions.
5. **Budget and tracking** – the daily budget from KES 9,000 a month (show the sum), an estimate of clicks using an example cost per click of KES 30, which landing page the ad would send people to, and one key event you would track (e.g. a click on the WhatsApp order button). Add two sentences on why your real results may differ from the estimate.

## Steps

1. Re-read the lessons on keywords, campaign structure, ad copy and budgets.
2. Decide the goal, then group Fatuma's services into ad groups.
3. Brainstorm keywords that show buying intent, then choose negatives.
4. Write and count your headlines.
5. Do the budget sums and name the tracking event.
6. Add one honest line about what you cannot promise Fatuma.

## What to submit

- Your plan in the text box **or** as a PDF/Word file (max 5 MB).
- Optional: a Google Sheets link (view access) for your keyword list.

## Tips

- Do not click real ads just to study them; each click costs the advertiser money.
- Keep headlines specific: "Birthday Cakes in Thika" beats "Best Cakes Ever".
- Never promise results such as "you will get 50 orders".
