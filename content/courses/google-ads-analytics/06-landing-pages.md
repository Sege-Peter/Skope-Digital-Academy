A click is not a customer. After someone clicks an ad, they arrive on a **landing page**. If that page is slow, confusing or does not match the ad, the money spent on the click is wasted. Many campaigns fail not because of the ads, but because of the landing page.

## What a landing page must do

A good landing page continues the conversation the ad started. If the ad says "Sofa Cleaning in Mombasa – Free Quote", the page should talk about **sofa cleaning in Mombasa** and make it easy to **get a free quote**. Sending people to a general home page and making them search is a common mistake.

## The landing page checklist

| Check | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Matches the ad** | The visitor immediately sees they are in the right place |
| **Loads fast on a phone** | Most Kenyan visitors use mobile data; slow pages lose people |
| **Clear headline** | Says what the business offers and where |
| **One main action** | Call, WhatsApp, fill a short form, or order – make it obvious |
| **Trust signals** | Real photos, reviews, physical address, years in business, payment methods |
| **Prices or "from" prices** | Where possible, reduces time-wasting enquiries |
| **Short forms** | Ask only what you need, such as name, phone and service |
| **Contact options** | Tap-to-call button and WhatsApp link that work on a phone |

## Example layout

```text
[Headline]  Sofa & Carpet Cleaning in Mombasa – We Come to You
[Sub-line]  Deep cleaning with safe products. Same-week bookings.
[Button]    WhatsApp for a Free Quote     [Button] Call Now
[Photos]    3 before/after pictures of real jobs
[Benefits]  - Dries in a few hours  - Vetted, trained staff  - Pay by M-Pesa after the job
[Reviews]   2-3 genuine customer reviews (with permission)
[Areas]     Nyali, Bamburi, Mombasa Island, Likoni
[Form]      Name | Phone | Number of seats | Preferred date | [Send]
```

## Speed matters

You can test a page's speed with Google's free **PageSpeed Insights** tool. Common fixes: compress large photos, remove heavy sliders and unnecessary plugins, and use good hosting. Even if you are not a web developer, you can spot these problems and advise the client.

## Policy and honesty

Google requires landing pages to be functional, useful and honest. Pages that do not work, hide important information, or promise things the business does not deliver can lead to disapproved ads. Make sure:

- Every link and button works.
- Prices and offers match what the ad says.
- The page clearly shows who the business is and how to contact them.
- If the page collects personal data, it explains how the data is used (a privacy notice).

> **Tip:** Before launching ads, open the landing page on a cheap Android phone using mobile data, not office Wi-Fi. That is the real experience of many visitors.

## No website? Other options

Some small businesses have no website. Options include sending clicks to a simple one-page site, a Google Business Profile, or using a call-focused campaign so people phone directly. Each option has trade-offs in what you can measure. Choose based on how the business actually closes sales.

## Practice task

Open the website of a local business by searching for it and clicking its normal (unpaid) result – do not click ads just to study them, because each click costs the advertiser money. Score the page using the checklist above, out of 8, on your phone using mobile data. Write the three changes that would most improve it.
