A search ad is small: a few headlines, a short description and a link. With so little space, every word must earn its place. Good ad copy matches the search, gives a reason to click and tells people what to do.

## Parts of a text search ad

The main format for search campaigns today is the **responsive search ad**. You write several headlines and descriptions, and Google tests combinations to show the best mix for each search.

| Part | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Headlines** | Short lines shown at the top of the ad, in blue | You write several; Google shows a few at a time |
| **Descriptions** | Longer lines below the headlines | Explain benefits and the offer |
| **Final URL** | The exact page people land on | Must work and match the ad |
| **Display path** | Optional words shown in the web address | e.g. `/sofa-cleaning` to show relevance |
| **Assets** | Extras like call buttons, location, extra links | Make the ad bigger and more useful |

> **Note:** Google sets character limits for headlines and descriptions (headlines are very short – around 30 characters at the time of writing). Check the current limits in the Google Ads Help Centre, and let the platform's counter guide you.

## A formula for headlines

Write headlines in different "flavours" so Google has good options to mix:

1. **Keyword headline** – repeat what they searched: "Sofa Cleaning in Mombasa"
2. **Benefit headline** – "Dry in a Few Hours"
3. **Trust headline** – "Insured, Vetted Cleaners"
4. **Offer headline** – "Free Quote on WhatsApp"
5. **Action headline** – "Book Your Clean Today"

## Example ad

```text
Headline 1: Sofa Cleaning in Mombasa
Headline 2: We Come to Your Home
Headline 3: Get a Free Quote Today
Description 1: Deep cleaning for sofas and carpets using safe products. Same-week bookings in Nyali, Bamburi and Mombasa Island.
Description 2: Trained, vetted team. Pay by M-Pesa after the job. Call or WhatsApp to book.
Display URL: yourbusiness.co.ke/sofa-cleaning
```

This is an invented example business for practice.

## Tips for strong copy

- **Match the keyword.** People are more likely to click when they see the words they typed.
- **Be specific.** "Delivered in 2 hours within Kisumu CBD" beats "Fast delivery".
- **Give a clear call to action.** Call, book, get a quote, order.
- **Highlight what makes the business different** – location, opening hours, payment options, guarantees that are real.
- **Write several versions** and let results show which work better.

## Rules you must follow

Google has advertising policies, and ads that break them are disapproved. Common problems for beginners:

- Excessive capital letters or symbols: "BEST!!! CHEAP!!!"
- Claims you cannot prove: "Number 1 in Kenya" without evidence.
- Misleading offers: advertising a price that is not available on the landing page.
- Restricted products and services: some categories (for example health, finance, alcohol, gambling) have extra rules or are not allowed. Always read the current policies.

## Ad assets

Assets (formerly called extensions) add useful extras: a call button, the business location, links to other pages (for example "Prices", "Contact"), or short highlights like "Open Sundays". They are often free to add – you still pay per click as usual.
