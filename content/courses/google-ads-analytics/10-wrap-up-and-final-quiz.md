Congratulations on reaching the end of the course. Here is a quick recap before the final quiz.

## What you learned

| Topic | Key idea |
|---|---|
| Search ads | People searching have intent; advertisers usually pay per click |
| The auction | Ad position depends on bid, ad quality, context and assets – not money alone |
| Keywords | Choose action and comparing searches; use match types and negative keywords |
| Structure | Account → campaigns (budget, location) → ad groups (one theme each) |
| Ad copy | Responsive search ads: keyword, benefit, trust, offer and action headlines |
| Budgets and bidding | Daily budget, manual vs automated bidding, CPC, conversion rate, cost per conversion |
| Landing pages | Match the ad, load fast on phones, one main action, trust signals |
| Analytics | Users, sessions, events, engagement, traffic sources, UTM links, key events |
| Reports | Compare, look for results not clicks, recommend clear actions |
| Ethics | No guarantees, no misleading ads, no click fraud, client owns the accounts |
