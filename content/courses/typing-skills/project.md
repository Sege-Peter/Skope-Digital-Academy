## The situation

Achieng wants a data-entry job and the employer says: "Show me proof of your typing speed and accuracy, and a sample of your work." In this project you will build a small **typing evidence pack** that a stranger can check from your files alone: screenshots of timed tests, a typed passage and a data-entry sample with made-up data. No trust required.

> **Note:** Use the practice text and made-up data given here. Do **not** type or upload real names, ID numbers, phone numbers or M-Pesa messages of other people. Hide any personal details that appear in screenshots.

## What you will produce

1. **Before and after test results** – a screenshot of a free one-minute online typing test from the start of your practice, and one from the end. Each must show the **date or time if visible, gross WPM, net WPM (or WPM) and accuracy percentage**. Use the same test site both times.
2. **Typed passage** – type the text below (about 110 words) exactly, without looking at your hands, and paste it in the text box. A peer will compare it with the original, so punctuation counts.
3. **Data-entry sample** – type the 12-row table below into a Google Sheet, Excel or Word table, with correct amounts and a total row for the amount column.
4. **Progress log** – a table with at least 5 days of practice (date, minutes, best net WPM, accuracy) and a 60–100 word reflection on your problem keys and what you changed.

## Steps

1. Take the first test today and screenshot the result before practising.
2. Practise daily with the 20-minute routine and fill in your log.
3. Type the passage and the table slowly and accurately first, then check them line by line.
4. Take the final test with the same site and screenshot it.
5. Combine everything and submit.

## What to submit

- Screenshots (PNG or JPG, max 5 MB each) or one PDF containing both test results, plus the passage, log and reflection in the text box.
- A link to your Google Sheet (set to "anyone with the link can view") or an uploaded Word or PDF file of your table.

## Tips

- Accuracy first: a clean 25 WPM beats a messy 40 WPM.
- Screenshot the whole result, not just the number.
- Do not pretend improvement. Reviewers check your numbers against your log.

```rubric
Test screenshots | Two clear screenshots from the same site, showing speed and accuracy, with an improvement or an honest note on why not | 5
Typed passage | Matches the original with at most a few errors in spelling, capitals and punctuation | 5
Data-entry sample | All twelve rows correct, with correct amounts and a correct total | 5
Progress log and reflection | At least five days logged and a 60–100 word reflection on problem keys | 3
Presentation | Files open, are readable and contain no real personal data | 2
```
