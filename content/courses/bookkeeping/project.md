## The situation

Achieng runs a small kiosk in Kisumu selling airtime, bread, milk, sugar and soap. She has never kept proper books. Everything is in her head and in scattered M-Pesa messages. She has asked you to keep her records for one month and show her how the business did. Below is her (made-up) month. Your job is to turn it into clean books.

> **Note:** Use only the made-up figures below. Do not use a real person's M-Pesa statement, phone number, ID number or real shop records. If you share a link, check that it does not show your own private data.

```text
1 Mar  Owner puts in cash to start         10,000
2 Mar  Bought stock (cash)                  4,000
3 Mar  Cash sales                           1,800
5 Mar  Paid kiosk rent                      1,500
7 Mar  Cash sales                           2,200
9 Mar  Bought stock (cash)                  2,500
12 Mar Cash sales                           2,600
15 Mar Paid electricity                       400
18 Mar Cash sales                           3,100
20 Mar Paid helper's wages                  1,200
25 Mar Cash sales                           2,900
28 Mar Bought stock (cash)                  3,000
30 Mar Owner takes cash for personal use      800
```

## What you will produce

One spreadsheet or notebook document with four parts:

1. **Cash book** – all 13 transactions with date, details, money in, money out and a running balance. Show the closing balance.
2. **Categories** – a small table totalling sales, stock purchases and each other expense.
3. **Double entry** – for any five transactions, write the debit and the credit, then post them to T-accounts for Cash and one other account.
4. **Summary for Achieng** – 3 to 5 sentences in plain English: total sales, total expenses, money left, and one piece of advice.

## Steps

1. Set up the cash book columns from the lessons and enter every transaction in date order.
2. Fill the running balance and check it using "opening + in - out".
3. Total each category (use SUMIF in Sheets or Excel, or add by hand).
4. Do the double entry and T-accounts for your five chosen transactions and balance them.
5. Write the summary as if Achieng will read it, with no jargon.

## What to submit

- A Google Sheets link (set to "anyone with the link can view") **or** a PDF, photo or Word file of your books (max 5 MB).
- The text box can hold your summary and any notes.

## Tips

- Keep money the owner takes out separate from business expenses.
- If your closing balance seems wrong, add up all "in" and all "out" again.
- A neat, simple layout is better than a fancy one.

```rubric
Cash book accuracy | All 13 transactions are entered correctly in the right columns with a correct running balance | 6
Categories | Sales, stock and each expense are totalled correctly and the totals match the cash book | 4
Double entry | Five transactions have the right debit and credit and are posted to balanced T-accounts | 4
Summary for the owner | The summary states sales, expenses, money left and one sensible piece of advice in plain English | 4
Neatness | Headings, dates and amounts are clear and easy to follow | 2
```
