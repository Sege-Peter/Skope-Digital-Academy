The cash book shows the money. But a business owner also wants to know *what* was sold, *who* owes money, and *where* the money is being spent. That is the job of the sales and expense records.

## The sales record (sales day book)

Record each sale, or each day's total sales, with enough detail to be useful.

| Date | Customer / details | Items | Cash/M-Pesa (KES) | Credit (KES) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Jul | Walk-in customers | 12 loaves, 6 cakes | 2,340 | |
| 1 Jul | Mama Otieno's shop | 30 loaves | | 1,800 |
| 2 Jul | Walk-in customers | 15 loaves, 4 cakes | 2,100 | |
| 2 Jul | Grace Academy | 2 large cakes | 3,000 | |
| | **Totals** | | **7,440** | **1,800** |

Total sales for the two days = 7,440 + 1,800 = **KES 9,240**.

**Credit sales** are sales where the customer will pay later. Only the cash and M-Pesa sales go into the cash book today; the credit sale goes into the cash book when Mama Otieno actually pays.

## The debtors list

Keep a page for customers who owe you (your **debtors**):

| Customer | Invoice | Date | Amount owed | Paid | Balance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mama Otieno's shop | 0051 | 1 Jul | 1,800 | 1,000 (8 Jul) | 800 |

Review it every week and follow up politely on old balances.

## The expense record

Group expenses into **categories** so you can see where money goes.

| Date | Details | Ref | Stock / ingredients | Rent | Transport | Utilities | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Jul | Flour, sugar, yeast | R-301 | 2,500 | | | | |
| 1 Jul | Monthly rent | R-302 | | 5,000 | | | |
| 2 Jul | Boda to market | Note | | | 150 | | |
| 3 Jul | Electricity tokens | QJK… | | | | 1,000 | |
| 3 Jul | Packaging bags | R-303 | | | | | 400 |
| | **Totals** | | **2,500** | **5,000** | **150** | **1,000** | **400** |

Total expenses = 2,500 + 5,000 + 150 + 1,000 + 400 = **KES 9,050**.

> **Tip:** Keep the categories few and consistent. Five to eight categories are enough for most small businesses. "Other" should stay small – if it grows, create a new category.

## Owner's drawings are not expenses

If Fatuma takes KES 500 of business money for her own lunch, record it as **drawings**, not as an expense. Mixing them makes the business look less profitable than it really is.

## Why separate records help

| Question | Where to look |
|---|---|
| What were total sales this week? | Sales record |
| Who owes me money? | Debtors list |
| Am I spending too much on transport? | Expense record, transport column |
| How much money do I have? | Cash book |

```exercise
Q: Cash sales are KES 5,200 and credit sales are KES 1,300. What are total sales?
Total sales = KES ___
A: 6500 / 6,500
```

```exercise
Q: A customer owed KES 1,800 and has paid KES 1,000. What is the balance owed?
Balance = KES ___

```
