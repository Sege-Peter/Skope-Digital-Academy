A **source document** is the original proof that a transaction happened. Bookkeepers record from source documents, not from memory. If there is no document, there should at least be a note explaining the entry.

## Common source documents

| Document | What it shows | Who issues it |
|---|---|---|
| **Invoice** | A request for payment: what was supplied, how much is owed and when to pay | The seller, before payment (or on credit sales) |
| **Receipt** | Proof that payment was received | The seller, after payment |
| **Cash sale receipt** | A sale paid immediately | The seller |
| **Delivery note** | Goods delivered (quantities, no prices needed) | The seller or transporter |
| **M-Pesa message or statement** | Mobile money received or sent | The mobile money provider |
| **Bank statement** | Money in and out of a bank account | The bank |
| **Credit note** | A reduction of what a customer owes (e.g. returned goods) | The seller |

## What a good invoice includes

```text
INVOICE                                  No. 0042
Achieng Designs, Kondele, Kisumu   Tel 07XX XXX XXX
Date: 5 May

Bill to: Grace Academy, Kisumu

Item                          Qty   Unit price    Amount
School event posters (A3)      20         150      3,000
Banner design                   1       2,500      2,500
                                       Total (KES) 5,500

Payment due: 19 May
Pay via M-Pesa Till 000000 or bank transfer.
```

Key parts: the word **INVOICE**, a unique **number**, date, seller and buyer details, items with quantities and prices, **total**, due date and how to pay.

> **Tip:** Number invoices and receipts in order (0041, 0042, 0043…). A missing number quickly shows that a document was lost or not recorded.

## What a good receipt includes

```text
RECEIPT                                  No. 0118
Achieng Designs                          Date: 18 May
Received from: Grace Academy
Amount: KES 5,500 (Five thousand five hundred shillings)
For: Invoice No. 0042
Paid by: M-Pesa, ref QEX12ABC34
Signed: (signature)
```

The receipt links back to the invoice it pays, so anyone can see that invoice 0042 is now settled.

## Electronic tax invoices

Some businesses are required by KRA to issue invoices through an approved electronic system. Whether this applies to a business, and how, depends on current KRA rules – check [kra.go.ke](https://www.kra.go.ke) rather than relying on hearsay.

## Filing your documents

- Use one envelope or box file per month, with "Sales" and "Expenses" sections.
- Take a photo of every paper receipt; thermal paper fades.
- Name scanned files clearly, e.g. `2025-05-18_receipt-0118_grace-academy.jpg`.
- Keep records for as long as the law requires – check the current period on kra.go.ke.
