## A combined worked example

Kiprono sells second-hand school bags in Eldoret. He buys a bale of 50 bags for KES 15,000 and pays KES 1,000 transport.

```text
1. Cost per bag (division)
   (15,000 + 1,000) ÷ 50 = KES 320

2. Price for a 60% markup (percentages)
   320 × 1.60 = KES 512 → he rounds to KES 500 to make change easy

3. Margin at KES 500 (profit ÷ selling price)
   Profit = 500 − 320 = 180;  180 ÷ 500 × 100 = 36%

4. Back-to-school discount of 10% (multiplier)
   500 × 0.90 = KES 450 → profit per bag falls to 450 − 320 = 130

5. Break-even on the bale at the discount price (algebra)
   450n = 16,000  →  n = 35.6 → he must sell 36 bags to cover costs

6. Estimate profit if he sells all 50 at KES 450
   50 × 450 − 16,000 = 22,500 − 16,000 = KES 6,500
```

## Formula summary sheet

| Topic | Formula / method |
|---|---|
| Percentage of an amount | amount × percentage ÷ 100 |
| What percentage? | part ÷ whole × 100 |
| Percentage change | (new − old) ÷ old × 100 |
| Increase / decrease by r% | × (1 + r/100) / × (1 − r/100) |
| Reverse percentage | final ÷ multiplier |
| Profit | selling price − cost |
| Markup / Margin | profit ÷ cost × 100 / profit ÷ selling price × 100 |
| Price for a target margin | cost ÷ (1 − margin) |
| Break-even units | fixed costs ÷ profit per unit |
| Simple interest | I = P × r × t |
| Compound amount | A = P × (1 + r)ⁿ |
| Sharing in a ratio a : b | total ÷ (a + b) × each part |
| Mean | total ÷ count |
| Speed | distance ÷ time |

> **Tip:** Copy this table into your notebook or phone. Many aptitude tests for jobs and college entry test exactly these skills.
