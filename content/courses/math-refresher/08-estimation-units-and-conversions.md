Quick estimates help you catch mistakes before they cost money, and correct units keep your measurements, quotes and orders accurate. This lesson brings both together.

## Estimation: round, then calculate

Round each number to something easy, then calculate in your head.

```text
48 crates × KES 205 per crate
≈ 50 × 200 = KES 10,000
Exact: 48 × 205 = KES 9,840 – close to the estimate ✔
```

If a calculator or invoice says KES 98,400, your estimate instantly tells you something is wrong (an extra zero).

### Estimating a shopping basket

```text
Item          Price    Rounded
Cooking oil     389      390
Rice 2 kg       298      300
Sugar 1 kg      162      160
Soap            118      120
Estimate               = 970  (exact: 967)
```

> **Tip:** Before paying any bill or sending money, do a quick estimate. It takes five seconds and catches typing errors such as KES 15,000 instead of KES 1,500.

## Metric units

| Measure | Conversions |
|---|---|
| Length | 1 km = 1,000 m · 1 m = 100 cm · 1 cm = 10 mm |
| Mass | 1 tonne = 1,000 kg · 1 kg = 1,000 g |
| Volume | 1 L = 1,000 mL · 1 m³ = 1,000 L |
| Time | 1 hour = 60 minutes · 1 day = 24 hours |

**Bigger unit → smaller unit: multiply. Smaller unit → bigger unit: divide.**

```text
2.5 kg  = 2.5 × 1,000 = 2,500 g
750 mL  = 750 ÷ 1,000 = 0.75 L
1.5 hours = 1.5 × 60  = 90 minutes
```

## Area and land

Area of a rectangle = length × width, in **square units**.

```text
A plot 50 m × 30 m = 1,500 m²
```

| Land unit | Size |
|---|---|
| 1 hectare (ha) | 10,000 m² |
| 1 acre | about 4,047 m² (about 0.405 ha) |
| 1 hectare | about 2.47 acres |

So the 1,500 m² plot = 1,500 ÷ 10,000 = **0.15 ha**. Land in Kenya is often sold in acres or fractions such as "an eighth of an acre" (about 506 m²).

## Speed, distance and time

```text
Speed = distance ÷ time     Distance = speed × time     Time = distance ÷ speed
```

A bus travels from Nairobi to Nakuru, about 160 km, at an average 64 km/h: time = 160 ÷ 64 = **2.5 hours** (2 hours 30 minutes).

## Fuel and trip costs

A car uses 8 litres per 100 km. A 350 km trip needs:

```text
Fuel = 350 ÷ 100 × 8 = 28 litres
At an example price of KES 180 per litre: 28 × 180 = KES 5,040
```

Fuel prices change monthly – use the current pump price for real planning.

## Currency conversion

Freelancers paid in US dollars convert using the current exchange rate, which changes daily. With an **example** rate of 1 USD = KES 130:

```text
USD → KES: multiply   USD 25 × 130 = KES 3,250
KES → USD: divide     KES 6,500 ÷ 130 = USD 50
```

Banks and payment services also add fees or use a slightly worse rate than the one on the news, so the amount you actually receive is usually a little lower. Check the real rate and charges on the day you withdraw.
