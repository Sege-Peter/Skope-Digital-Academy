TikTok helps a business get **discovered** by new people. WhatsApp helps a business **talk to and sell to** people who are already interested. Used together, they form a powerful pair for small businesses in Kenya.

## TikTok: short videos, big reach

TikTok shows people videos from accounts they do not follow yet, based on what they watch. This means a brand-new business account can reach many people if a video is interesting enough.

What makes a TikTok video work:

- **A strong first two seconds.** Start with movement, a question or a surprising result: "This KES 300 trick saved my sufuria."
- **Vertical, clear and well lit.** Film near a window or outside in daylight.
- **Real people and real places.** Customers trust a genuine shop in Kitengela more than a polished studio.
- **Captions on screen.** Many people watch without sound.
- **One idea per video.** Keep it short and focused.

Video ideas for local businesses:

| Business | Video idea |
|---|---|
| Kinyozi | Before and after of a fresh cut, sped up |
| Mama mboga | "What KES 200 buys you today" |
| Phone repair | Screen replacement in 30 seconds |
| Tailor | Fabric to finished dress, step by step |
| Tutor | One quick maths trick for KCSE revision |

Switch the account to a **business account** to see analytics such as views, watch time and follower growth. Use music and sounds that the platform marks as allowed for commercial use; popular songs may not be licensed for business accounts.

## WhatsApp Business: where sales happen

**WhatsApp Business** is a free app made for small businesses. It looks like normal WhatsApp but adds useful tools:

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Business profile | Shows address, hours, description, email and website |
| Catalogue | Lists products with photos, prices and descriptions |
| Greeting message | Automatic welcome when someone messages for the first time |
| Away message | Automatic reply outside working hours |
| Quick replies | Saved answers you insert with a shortcut, e.g. `/prices` |
| Labels | Tag chats such as "New order", "Paid", "Delivered" |
| Broadcast lists | Send one message to many contacts individually |

> **Note:** Broadcast messages only reach people who have **saved your number** in their phone. Encourage customers to save the business number.

## Good WhatsApp manners

1. Reply quickly. A slow reply often means a lost sale.
2. Confirm orders in writing: item, size, price, delivery place and time.
3. Share the business M-Pesa till or paybill number clearly, and confirm payment before dispatch.
4. Do not add people to groups without asking. Do not spam broadcasts every day.
5. Use the **Status** for daily stock updates – many customers check statuses regularly.

## Linking TikTok to WhatsApp

Put a WhatsApp click-to-chat link in the TikTok bio (when the platform allows a link) or tell viewers on screen: "WhatsApp 07XX XXX XXX to order." WhatsApp provides links in the form `wa.me/` followed by the number in international format without the plus sign, such as `wa.me/2547XXXXXXXX`.

> **Warning:** Never share an M-Pesa PIN, a WhatsApp verification code or a social media password with anyone, including "customers" or people claiming to be from Safaricom or Meta. Fraudsters use these to steal accounts.
