When someone in Mombasa types "tyre shop near me" into Google, the businesses that appear first get most of the calls. **SEO (Search Engine Optimisation)** is the work of helping a business appear in search results for free, without paying for ads.

## How search works (simply)

1. Google's programs visit and read web pages across the internet.
2. Google stores what each page is about.
3. When someone searches, Google tries to show the most **relevant** and **trustworthy** results for that search and that person's location.

Nobody outside Google knows every detail of how results are ranked, and nobody can honestly guarantee a "number one" position. But the basics below are widely accepted good practice.

## Start with Google Business Profile

For local businesses, the most valuable free step is a **Google Business Profile**. It is the listing that can appear on Google Maps and in local search results with the business name, location, phone number, hours, photos and reviews.

To make a profile strong:

- Use the real business name, exact location and a working phone number.
- Choose the most accurate business category.
- Add opening hours and keep them updated on holidays.
- Upload real photos of the shop front, inside and products.
- Ask happy customers for reviews, and reply politely to every review – including negative ones.

> **Warning:** Do not write fake reviews or pay for them. Google may remove them or penalise the listing, and customers lose trust when they spot fakes.

## Keywords

**Keywords** are the words people type into search. Think like the customer:

| Business | Possible keywords |
|---|---|
| Driving school in Kisumu | driving school Kisumu, driving lessons Kisumu, learn to drive Kisumu |
| Event tent hire | tent hire Nakuru, chairs and tents for hire, wedding tents Nakuru |
| Plumber | plumber near me, plumber Ruiru, blocked sink repair |

Use these words naturally in the business description, website pages and posts. Do not stuff the same word again and again – write for people first.

## On-page SEO for websites

If the business has a website, each page should have:

- A clear **title tag** (the clickable blue headline in search results), for example "Tent and Chair Hire in Nakuru | Baraka Events".
- A **meta description** – one or two sentences under the title that encourage the click.
- Headings and text that clearly describe the service and location.
- Fast loading and a design that works well on phones – most Kenyan users browse on mobile.
- Image descriptions (alt text) that say what the picture shows.

## Off-page signals

Other websites linking to the business (called **backlinks**), consistent name-address-phone details across directories, and genuine reviews all help Google trust a business. Getting listed on reputable local directories and being mentioned by local news or partner sites can help.

## SEO takes time

Social posts can get attention in hours. SEO usually takes weeks or months to show results. Set honest expectations with clients and keep working steadily: updated profile, new photos, more reviews, helpful content.
