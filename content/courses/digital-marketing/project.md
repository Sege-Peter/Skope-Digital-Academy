## The situation

Fatuma runs "Fatuma's Braids and Beauty", a small salon in Kisii. It has a Facebook Page and a WhatsApp number but posts only now and then, and new customers are few. You have been asked to plan two weeks of marketing. The salon and all details are made up, so you can use your own imagination to fill gaps. Alternatively, plan for another made-up business of your choice.

> **Note:** Use made-up names, phone numbers and prices. Do not include real customers' photos, messages or numbers, and do not copy a real business's private details.

## What you will produce

One document with five parts:

1. **Audience and promise** – one persona (name, age, town, apps, wants, worries) and a one-sentence value proposition.
2. **Content calendar** – two weeks with at least 8 posts. Columns: date, platform, content pillar, idea, and call to action. Include one post tied to a holiday or school date.
3. **Two captions** – write one product or service caption and one offer caption using the AIDA method, each with a hook and one clear call to action. Add a version under 20 words for WhatsApp Status.
4. **WhatsApp Business greeting** – a greeting message under 50 words.
5. **How you will measure** – a table with three numbers you will track (for example reach, clicks and messages) and what a good result would look like in two weeks.

## Steps

1. Reread your notes on target audience, content pillars and copywriting.
2. Write the persona and value proposition.
3. Fill in the calendar, spreading posts across at least two platforms.
4. Write the captions and greeting. Read them aloud.
5. Add your measurement table and proofread.

## What to submit

- Your plan in the text box **or** as a PDF, Word file, or PNG/JPG (max 5 MB).
- Optional: a Canva or Google Sheets link with a mock-up post.

## Tips

- Use your pillars, so the plan does not become a list of random posts.
- Every caption needs one clear next step, such as "Send us a message on WhatsApp".
- Do not promise discounts or results that the business has not agreed to.
