Marketing without measuring is guessing. Every platform you have learned about gives you numbers. Your job is to pick the numbers that matter, read them correctly and use them to improve.

## Start with a goal

Before looking at numbers, ask: **what does the business actually want?** More followers is rarely the real goal. Real goals sound like:

- More WhatsApp enquiries per week.
- More bookings for the salon on weekdays.
- More people visiting the shop after seeing a post.
- More sign-ups for a training.

Choose the numbers that show progress towards that goal.

## Common metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Reach** | How many different people saw the content |
| **Impressions** | How many times the content was shown in total (one person can see it several times) |
| **Engagement** | Likes, comments, shares, saves and similar actions |
| **Engagement rate** | Engagement compared with reach (or followers), as a percentage |
| **Clicks** | How many times people tapped a link or button |
| **CTR (click-through rate)** | Clicks ÷ impressions × 100 |
| **Conversions** | People who did the action you want: messaged, booked, bought |
| **Cost per result** | For paid ads: amount spent ÷ number of results |

> **Note:** Each platform defines some metrics slightly differently. Always check the platform's own explanation when you report to a client.

## Simple calculations

Engagement rate (using reach):

```text
Engagement rate = engagements ÷ reach × 100
Example: 60 engagements ÷ 1,200 reach × 100 = 5%
```

Click-through rate:

```text
CTR = clicks ÷ impressions × 100
Example: 30 clicks ÷ 3,000 impressions × 100 = 1%
```

Cost per result:

```text
Cost per result = amount spent ÷ results
Example: KES 1,500 spent ÷ 30 WhatsApp chats = KES 50 per chat
```

These figures are examples only. Results vary hugely by business, audience and season.

```exercise
Q: A post got 4,000 impressions and 80 clicks. Calculate the CTR.
CTR = 80 ÷ 4,000 × 100 = ___%
A: 2
```

```exercise
Q: A client spent KES 2,000 on an ad and got 40 messages. What was the cost per message?
Cost per message = KES ___
A: 50 / KES 50
H: Divide the amount spent by the number of messages.
```

## Vanity metrics vs useful metrics

**Vanity metrics** look nice but do not pay bills: a big follower count, or a video with many views but no enquiries. Useful metrics connect to money or the goal: chats, bookings, sales, sign-ups.

A simple way to link posts to sales is to **ask customers** "Where did you hear about us?" and write the answer down, or use a special word: "Mention TIKTOK10 when ordering."

## The improve loop

1. **Plan** – set a goal and decide what to measure.
2. **Post** – run the content or campaign.
3. **Measure** – collect the numbers weekly.
4. **Learn** – which posts did best? Which times? Which formats?
5. **Adjust** – do more of what works, stop what does not.

## A simple monthly report

Clients value a short, honest report:

```text
Month: March | Client: Baraka Events, Nakuru
Goal: more enquiries for tent hire
- WhatsApp enquiries: 46 (February: 31)
- Best post: reel of a wedding setup (reach 5,400, 22 chats)
- Weakest: price list graphic (few saves, no chats)
- Next month: 2 more setup reels, test a Saturday morning post time
```

## Practice task

Open the insights of any account you manage (personal professional account or practice business). Record reach, engagement and link clicks for your last five posts in a table. Calculate the engagement rate for each and write two lessons you learn from the best and worst post.
