If you try to talk to everyone, you end up talking to no one. Your **target audience** is the specific group of people most likely to buy from a business. Knowing them well makes every post, advert and message easier to write.

## Describing your audience

Marketers describe an audience in two ways.

| Type | What it covers | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Demographics** | Facts about the person | Age, gender, location, income level, job, student or working |
| **Psychographics** | What they care about | Interests, worries, goals, values, habits, favourite platforms |

"Everyone in Kenya" is not an audience. "Parents of primary school children in Kisumu who worry about school fees and shop on a budget" is an audience.

## Build a customer persona

A **persona** is a short, imaginary profile of one typical customer. Giving them a name helps you picture a real person while you write.

```text
Persona: "Brian the Boda Rider"
Age: 22-30, lives in Eldoret
Works: boda boda rider, earns daily
Phone: Android, uses WhatsApp and TikTok most
Wants: affordable phone repairs done fast, so he does not lose work
Worries: being overcharged, fake spare parts
Best time to reach: early morning and evening
```

A phone repair shop that knows Brian will post short TikTok videos showing quick repairs, mention warranty on parts, and highlight evening opening hours.

## Where do you get this information?

You do not need expensive research. Start with:

- **Talking to customers.** Ask existing buyers how they found the business and why they chose it.
- **Watching comments and messages.** What questions keep coming up? What complaints?
- **Looking at competitors.** Who comments on their posts? What do people praise or complain about?
- **Platform insights.** Business pages on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok show basic data about followers, such as age range and location.

## The marketing message

Once you know the audience, write one clear sentence that says why they should choose this business. This is often called a **value proposition**.

A simple formula:

```text
We help [audience] get [result] without [problem they worry about].
```

Examples:

- "We help Eldoret riders get their phones fixed in under an hour, with genuine parts and a warranty."
- "We help busy Nairobi mothers get fresh vegetables delivered to the door, without going to the market."

> **Tip:** Talk about **benefits**, not only **features**. "6,000 mAh battery" is a feature. "Lasts two full days, so you won't miss customers" is a benefit.

## One business can have several audiences

A school uniform shop in Thika might serve parents (who pay) and schools (who order in bulk). Each group needs a different message: parents care about price and durability; schools care about consistent colours and delivery dates. Write a separate persona for each.
