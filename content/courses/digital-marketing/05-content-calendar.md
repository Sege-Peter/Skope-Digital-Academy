The businesses that grow online are usually not the ones with the most expensive phones. They are the ones that post **consistently**. A **content calendar** is a simple plan of what you will post, where and when. It stops the "what should I post today?" panic.

## Content pillars

**Content pillars** are 3–5 themes a business posts about again and again. They keep content varied but focused.

Example pillars for Wanjiru's salon in Nyeri:

| Pillar | Purpose | Example post |
|---|---|---|
| Showcase | Show the work | Before/after of a braiding style |
| Educate | Give useful tips | "How to keep your braids neat for 3 weeks" |
| Trust | Build confidence | Customer review with her permission |
| Behind the scenes | Show real people | Morning setup, meet the stylists |
| Offers | Drive sales | "Tuesday discount for students" (with clear terms) |

A common guideline is that most posts should **give value** (entertain, teach or inspire) and only some should directly **sell**. If every post says "buy now", people stop paying attention.

## Creating content on a phone

You can make good content with a phone and free apps:

- **Photos:** clean background, natural light, product in focus, the same style every time.
- **Graphics:** use Canva templates for offers, price lists and announcements.
- **Video:** use the phone camera and an editing app such as CapCut or the platform's own editor.
- **Batch create:** set aside one afternoon a week to shoot several photos and videos at once. It is faster than creating something new every day.

> **Tip:** Keep a folder of "evergreen" content – posts that are useful at any time, such as tips and FAQs. Use them on days when nothing new is happening.

## Building the calendar

A calendar can be a notebook, a Google Sheet or a free planning tool. The important columns are:

```text
Date     | Platform        | Pillar     | Format   | Idea / caption               | Status
Mon 3rd  | Instagram, FB   | Showcase   | Reel     | Knotless braids before/after  | Ready
Wed 5th  | WhatsApp Status | Offers     | Graphic  | Student Tuesday reminder      | Draft
Fri 7th  | TikTok          | Educate    | Video    | 3 tips for edges              | Idea
Sat 8th  | Instagram, FB   | Trust      | Carousel | Two customer reviews          | Idea
```

Plan around the **Kenyan calendar** too: school opening and closing dates, public holidays like Madaraka Day and Jamhuri Day, Easter, Christmas, Valentine's Day, and month-end when many people get paid.

## How often should you post?

There is no single correct number. It is better to post **3 times a week every week** than 10 times one week and nothing for a month. Choose a schedule the business can actually keep, then use the platform insights to adjust.

You can **schedule** posts in advance using Meta's business tools for Facebook and Instagram, or the scheduling options in other platforms. Scheduling lets you prepare a week's posts in one sitting.

## Respect copyright and privacy

- Do not take photos from Google or other businesses' pages and post them as yours.
- Ask permission before posting customers' faces, and be extra careful with photos of children.
- Use your own photos, free stock libraries that allow commercial use, or the client's own images.
