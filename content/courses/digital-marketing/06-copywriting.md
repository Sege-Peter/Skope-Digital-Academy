**Copywriting** is writing words that make people take action: read more, send a message, visit, or buy. Every caption, advert, flyer and product description is copy. Good copy is clear, short and focused on the reader.

## Write for one reader

Picture the persona you created in [Know Your Target Audience](target-audience). Write as if you are talking to that one person. Use "you" more than "we".

| Weak | Stronger |
|---|---|
| We are the best tailors in Kisumu. | Need your school uniform fitted before opening day? We finish in 48 hours. |
| We sell quality solar lamps. | Study at night without paraffin smoke. One charge lasts all evening. |

The stronger versions speak to a real need and give a reason to act.

## The AIDA formula

A classic structure for persuasive copy is **AIDA**:

1. **Attention** – a headline or first line that stops scrolling.
2. **Interest** – a detail that makes them want to know more.
3. **Desire** – show the benefit and how life improves.
4. **Action** – tell them exactly what to do next.

Example caption for a water delivery business in Machakos:

```text
Tired of carrying jerricans up the stairs? (Attention)
We deliver clean, treated 20-litre water to your door, including upper floors. (Interest)
No queues, no back pain – just fresh water when you need it. (Desire)
WhatsApp 07XX XXX XXX before 10am for same-day delivery. (Action)
```

## Headlines and hooks

The first line matters most because many people only read that. Hook ideas:

- A question: "Is your phone battery dying by lunchtime?"
- A number: "3 ways to make your uniform last the whole year"
- A result: "From cracked screen to brand new in 1 hour"
- A local touch: "Nakuru, your Saturday chapati plans are sorted."

## Call to action (CTA)

A **call to action** tells the reader the next step. Make it specific:

- Weak: "Contact us."
- Strong: "Tap the WhatsApp button and send the word CAKE to get our price list."

Only use one main CTA per post so people are not confused.

```

```

## Clear, simple language

- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Plain English (or Kiswahili / Sheng if that is how the audience speaks – match the audience).
- Check spelling and prices twice. A wrong price causes arguments.
- Use emojis lightly if they suit the brand.

## Be honest

Never promise what the business cannot deliver. Avoid fake "only 2 left" claims, fake reviews or miracle health claims. Kenya has consumer protection laws, and platforms remove misleading adverts. Honest copy builds customers who come back.

> **Tip:** Read your copy out loud. If it sounds awkward when spoken, rewrite it.

## Practice task

Write **two captions** for your practice business using AIDA: one for a product or service, one for an offer. Each must have a hook in the first line and one specific call to action. Then rewrite one of them in under 20 words for a WhatsApp Status.
