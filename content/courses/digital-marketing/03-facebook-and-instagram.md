Facebook and Instagram are both owned by Meta, and businesses usually manage them together. Facebook is popular with a wide age range in Kenya, including parents and older customers. Instagram is more visual and often attracts younger audiences interested in fashion, food, beauty and lifestyle.

## Personal profile vs business presence

Never run a business from a personal profile. Instead:

- On **Facebook**, create a **Page** for the business. A Page can have several admins, shows contact details and opening hours, and gives you insights about your audience.
- On **Instagram**, switch to a **professional account** (business or creator). This unlocks insights, contact buttons and the option to run ads.
- Connect the two accounts so you can manage messages and schedule posts in one place using Meta's business tools.

> **Note:** Menus and button names on these apps change often. If a setting is not where you expect, search the app's help centre for the feature name.

## Set up the profile properly

A complete profile builds trust. Check that the business has:

| Item | Good practice |
|---|---|
| Profile picture | The logo, clear even when small |
| Cover / header | A photo of the shop, products or team |
| Name and username | Same name on every platform if possible |
| Bio / About | What you sell, where you are, and how to order, in one or two lines |
| Contact | Phone, WhatsApp button, location pin, opening hours |
| Link | A WhatsApp chat link, website or catalogue |

Example Instagram bio for a bakery:

```text
Achieng's Bakes | Kisumu
Custom birthday & wedding cakes
Order 3 days ahead | Delivery in Kisumu town
Tap WhatsApp below to order
```

## Types of content that work

- **Photos** of products with price and sizes in the caption.
- **Reels** (short vertical videos): behind the scenes, before and after, how-to tips.
- **Carousels** (several images in one post): "5 hairstyles for school girls", "menu for this week".
- **Stories**: daily updates, polls, countdowns to an offer. They disappear after 24 hours, so they are good for casual, frequent posts.
- **Customer reviews** and photos (always ask permission first).

## Engagement: be social

Social media is a conversation. The algorithm tends to show more people posts that get comments, shares and saves. Help that happen:

1. Ask a simple question in the caption: "Which colour would you pick, red or black?"
2. Reply to every comment and message quickly and politely.
3. Use a few relevant hashtags, for example `#NairobiFashion` or `#KisumuEats`, not thirty random ones.
4. Post at times your audience is online. Check your insights to find them.

## Paid boosts and ads (a first look)

You can pay to show a post to more people. The simple option is "boosting" a post; the more powerful option is creating ads in Meta's ads tools, where you choose a goal (for example messages or website visits), an audience and a budget. Start with a small daily budget for a few days, check the results, then decide whether to continue. Never spend a client's money without their written approval of the budget.

> **Warning:** Scammers send fake "Meta Support" messages saying a Page will be deleted unless you log in through a link. Meta will not ask for your password in a message. Turn on two-factor authentication on every business account you manage.
